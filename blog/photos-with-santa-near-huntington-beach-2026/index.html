Photos With Santa Near Huntington Beach 2026: Hours, Prices and Reservations

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 25, 2026

Short answer: Four places near Huntington Beach have Santa for 2026, and none of them on Thanksgiving Day. In town, Pacific City has Santa in an Airstream by the ocean (free visit, photos from $44.99), and Bella Terra starts Friday, November 27. South Coast Plaza ($19 to $59) and Fashion Island ($44.99 to $64.99, walk-in only) run daily through December 24.

This guide is for a family staying in Huntington Beach over Thanksgiving or in December, where the grandkids are in town for a few days and somebody wants the Santa picture before everyone flies home. It tells you which Santa is open on the days you have, what it costs, and whether to book. Every date, hour and price below comes from the center's own Santa page or its booking calendar, read on November 25, 2026. Where something is not posted, we say so. Prices are before tax.

Where can we get photos with Santa near Huntington Beach in 2026?

Pacific City and Bella Terra are the two in Huntington Beach. South Coast Plaza and Fashion Island are bigger sets a short drive away. Santa visits themselves are free everywhere; you pay for the photo package.

Can we see Santa on Thanksgiving weekend?

Not on Thursday. Friday, Saturday and Sunday, yes, at all four. South Coast Plaza and Pacific City are closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, Fashion Island's center is closed that day, and Bella Terra's Santa does not arrive until Friday.

South Coast Plaza's Black Friday hours come from its holiday hours page; it warns that the Santa line may close before the center does.

What are Pacific City's Santa hours in 2026?

Pacific City has Santa on selected days only, mostly Thursday to Sunday, from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., then every day from December 18. The visit is free, Cherry Hill Programs sells the photos (from $44.99), and booking ahead gets a free personalized phone call from Santa.

  • Thanksgiving weekend: Fri Nov 27 to Sun Nov 29, 11 a.m.–6 p.m.
  • Thu Dec 3: 11 a.m.–5:15 p.m. (Pacific City lists a tree lighting event that evening)
  • Fri Dec 4 to Sun Dec 6 and Thu Dec 10 to Sun Dec 13: 11 a.m.–6 p.m.
  • Thu Dec 17: 11 a.m.–4 p.m. (Cookie Crawl event)
  • Fri Dec 18 to Wed Dec 23: 11 a.m.–6 p.m.
  • Christmas Eve, Thu Dec 24: 11 a.m.–5 p.m.

No Santa on Mondays to Wednesdays before December 21. The shorter December 3 and 17 hours are on Pacific City's Santa page; its Cherry Hill booking calendar still shows 11 to 6 on those two days, so trust the shorter time. The booking page says several families share each 15-minute slot, so a reservation is a short wait, not a private session. Pacific City says the Airstream overlooks the Pacific, which makes it the one set on this list with a beach-town feel.

What are Bella Terra's Santa hours in 2026?

Bella Terra has Santa every day from November 27 to December 24 on The Green, the lawn in front of Century Theatres. Weekday visits are late afternoon only. These times are from the center's Cherry Hill booking calendar:

If you are not sure which package you want, Bella Terra sells a $19.99 "Reserve Now, Decide Later" booking that counts toward any package on the day. Package prices are only shown in the booking flow, not on the center's page. Booking ahead also comes with 50% off a Shutterfly order, 10 free holiday cards and a free personalized message from Santa through Portable North Pole.

One warning: the "Santa Hours" PDF linked from Bella Terra's hours page shows a December where the 1st falls on a Monday. That is last year's calendar (December 1, 2026 is a Tuesday). Use the booking calendar above instead.

Is there a sensory-friendly Santa near Huntington Beach?

Yes. Bella Terra holds a Sensory-Friendly Santa on Sunday, December 6, 2026, from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m., before the regular line opens. It has less noise and distraction, more space and a coloring station. The visit is free and photos are for sale. Reservations are not required but strongly recommended: on the booking page choose "View Event Schedule," then Sensory-Friendly Santa, December 6. No personal photography is allowed at this session.

How much are Santa photos at South Coast Plaza and Fashion Island?

South Coast Plaza runs from $19 to $59. Fashion Island runs from $44.99 to $64.99. Both are walk-up, and neither lets you take your own photos.

South Coast Plaza lists eight packages, valid through December 24, 2026, plus tax:

  • $19: one 5x7 print
  • $29: one digital download and one 5x7 print (same image); or one 5x7, one 3x5 and two wallets (same image); or any 5x7 frame or snow globe with no prints
  • $39: two digital downloads and one 5x7 print
  • $48: a 5x7 frame and a 5x7 print; or a snow globe and four snow globe prints
  • $59: four digital downloads and one 5x7 print

Santa is in Carousel Court and the Macy's Home Store wing on Level 1, during all center hours (normally 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday to Thursday, 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Friday and Saturday, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Sunday). It is indoors, which makes it the safe choice on a rainy day. Pets are not allowed, except leashed service animals.

Fashion Island puts Santa in a coastal cottage on the Atrium Lawn. Hours are 10 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. on weekdays, 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Saturdays and 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sundays through December 12; 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily from December 13 to 20; 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. from December 21 to 23; and 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. on Christmas Eve. The center says hours depend on the weather. Packages:

  • $44.99 Basic: two 4x6 prints and four wallets
  • $49.99 Standard: adds digital downloads and 20 e-cards
  • $54.99 Premium: adds a Christmas print, a snow globe print, a 5x7 and 40 e-cards
  • $64.99 Elite: adds a third-pose 5x7 and unlimited e-cards

Fashion Island's tree lights up with falling snow every half hour from 5:30 to 8 p.m., so a 4:30 Santa visit followed by the snowfall makes one easy evening. Times are in our Christmas lights guide.

Can we take our own photos with Santa?

Usually not, so budget for a package. South Coast Plaza says personal cameras and videos are not allowed. Fashion Island asks you not to use your own camera or phone while visiting Santa. Bella Terra's sensory session says no personal photography. Pacific City's page does not say either way, so ask at the Airstream before you hand over a phone.

Is there a free Santa photo near Huntington Beach?

Not at the malls: the visit is free, the picture is not. The best free-to-attend option is the Snoopy House at Costa Mesa City Hall, December 11 to 18, 2026, where the city's 2026 flyer lists photos with Santa from 6 to 8 p.m. each night. We could not confirm whether those photos cost anything: the city's Snoopy House page would not load for us on November 25. Details are in our Christmas lights guide.

If you were hoping for the Santa train at Irvine Park in Orange, its 2026 Christmas Train is sold out and that is the only way to reach Santa there. Our Irvine Park Christmas Train guide covers the free Santa's Village and the Train of Lights, which goes on sale December 7.

Can we bring the dog?

Yes, at Pacific City. Its Santa Paws sessions run on Sundays from 4 to 6 p.m.: November 29 and December 6, 13 and 20, 2026. South Coast Plaza does not allow pets at Santa. Bella Terra and Fashion Island do not say.

What is the last day for Santa photos in 2026?

Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, at all four, with shorter hours. Fashion Island stops first.

  • Fashion Island: 9 a.m.–3 p.m.
  • Bella Terra: 10 a.m.–4 p.m.
  • South Coast Plaza: center open 10 a.m.–4 p.m.
  • Pacific City: 11 a.m.–5 p.m.

The last few days before Christmas are the busiest. If you can, go on a weekday in early December instead.

Santa photos near Huntington Beach FAQ

Where can I get photos with Santa in Huntington Beach in 2026?

Two places in Huntington Beach have Santa for 2026. Pacific City, 21010 Pacific Coast Highway, has Santa in a vintage Airstream on selected dates through December 24; the visit is free and photo packages start at $44.99. Bella Terra, 7777 Edinger Avenue, has Santa on The Green from November 27 to December 24, with reservations encouraged.

Is Santa at the mall on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

No, not near Huntington Beach. South Coast Plaza and Pacific City are closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, 2026, Fashion Island's center is closed that day, and Bella Terra's Santa starts on Friday, November 27. Friday to Sunday after Thanksgiving, all four have Santa.

How much are Santa photos at South Coast Plaza in 2026?

South Coast Plaza lists eight Santa photo packages for 2026, from $19 for one 5x7 print to $59 for four digital downloads and a 5x7 print, plus tax. Santa is in Carousel Court and the Macy's Home Store wing, Level 1, during center hours through December 24.

Do I need a reservation for Santa photos?

It depends on the place. Fashion Island is walk-in only with no reservations. Pacific City and Bella Terra recommend booking a time online, and walk-ups are taken as space allows. South Coast Plaza's Santa page does not mention reservations and says the line may close before the center closes.

Can I bring my dog to see Santa near Huntington Beach?

Yes, at Pacific City's Santa Paws, Sundays from 4 to 6 p.m. on November 29 and December 6, 13 and 20, 2026. South Coast Plaza does not allow pets at Santa, except leashed service animals.

Can I take my own photos with Santa on my phone?

Usually not. South Coast Plaza says personal cameras and videos are not allowed, Fashion Island asks guests not to use their own camera or phone, and Bella Terra's sensory-friendly session says no personal photography. Pacific City's page does not say, so ask at the Airstream.

After Santa: see the lights

Santa visits end before dark, and the lights start right after. Our free Christmas lights guide has the Pier snowflakes, Fashion Island's snowfall times and the Newport Dunes floating lights, and the Christmas in Huntington Beach guide covers the Cruise of Lights in Huntington Harbour.
