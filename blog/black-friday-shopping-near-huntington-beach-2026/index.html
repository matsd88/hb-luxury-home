Black Friday 2026 Near Huntington Beach: Mall Hours, Parking and Small Business Saturday

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 21, 2026

Short answer: Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. Near Huntington Beach, the earliest posted opening is The Outlets at Orange at 6 a.m. (to 10 p.m.). Fashion Island opens at 8 a.m., South Coast Plaza at 9 a.m., and Pacific City at 10 a.m. Citadel Outlets opens at 8 p.m. on Thanksgiving and stays open until 11 p.m. Friday. Most other malls are closed on Thanksgiving Day.

This guide is for a family staying in Huntington Beach over Thanksgiving who wants one good shopping day on Friday or Saturday: which center opens when, which ones are close, where to park, and how to avoid sitting on the 405. Every hour below comes from the center's own website, read on November 21, 2026. Where a center has not posted 2026 hours, we say so.

Which center should we pick?

Pick by what you want to buy and how early you want to be up. For deals at dawn, go to the outlets. For a nicer day with a holiday show, go to Fashion Island or South Coast Plaza. If you only have a couple of hours, stay in Huntington Beach.

  • Early risers and outlet hunters: The Outlets at Orange. Simon lists 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Black Friday and closed on Thanksgiving. It is the earliest posted opening in Orange County on our list. Its regular weekend hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays and 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Sundays.
  • Night owls: Citadel Outlets. Citadel's hours page lists 8 p.m. to midnight on Thanksgiving, then midnight to 11 p.m. on Friday, so it is open straight through the night. Saturday is 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. It is the farthest drive on this list, in Commerce, south-east of downtown Los Angeles. It only makes sense if your group wants a late-night outing after Thanksgiving dinner.
  • A slower day with a show: Fashion Island. The Black Friday page lists the center open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on November 27. It has free Lindt chocolate from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. in the Neiman Marcus and Bloomingdale's courtyard while supplies last, and a free wreath styling station on the Macy's lawn from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. (one per person). From 5:30 to 8 p.m. the 90-foot tree lights up with falling snow every half hour, weather permitting. It is an open-air center, so a jacket helps after dark.
  • Luxury and department stores: South Coast Plaza. Its holiday hours page lists Thanksgiving closed and November 27 from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays and 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Sundays. Department store and restaurant hours may differ.
  • Close to the house: Pacific City and Bella Terra. Both are in Huntington Beach. See the next section.

Is anything in Huntington Beach open on Black Friday?

Yes. Pacific City, across Pacific Coast Highway from the beach, is open 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday. Bella Terra had not posted 2026 holiday hours by November 21, so check before you go.

Pacific City. The center's hours page lists it closed on Thanksgiving, then 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28, and 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Sunday, November 29. It is the easy one to pair with a beach walk: shop for an hour, then cross to the sand for sunset, which is around 4:45 p.m. that week. Its next big date is the free Cool Yule tree lighting on Thursday, December 3, from 4 to 7 p.m., with the tree lit at about 6:05 p.m.

Bella Terra. This is the closest mall to the house, by the 405 at Edinger Avenue and Beach Boulevard. On November 21, the calendar on Bella Terra's hours page showed regular hours for Thanksgiving week. The holiday hours sheet it links to is last year's. That 2025 sheet had the center closed on Thanksgiving and open 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday, with store hours varying. We would plan on the same pattern for 2026, but treat it as a guess until the center posts this year's sheet. Its regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday to Saturday and 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday.

Is there a Small Business Saturday event on Main Street?

Small Business Saturday is Saturday, November 28, 2026, according to the U.S. Small Business Administration. On November 21, Downtown Huntington Beach had not posted a Small Business Saturday event.

That does not stop you from shopping small. Main Street between Pacific Coast Highway and Orange Avenue is lined with independent surf shops, boutiques and cafes, and it is a short walk from the Pier. Each shop sets its own hours, so the morning is the safest time. The Downtown Business Improvement District does list Magic on Main Street on Tuesday, November 24, from 4 to 7 p.m., with no other details posted. That is the same Tuesday evening as Surf City Nights, the weekly street fair and farmers market. If you are in town early in the week, Tuesday evening is the best night for Main Street.

Where do we park on Black Friday?

At the malls, use the parking structures and arrive before noon. Downtown, the Main Promenade structure at 200 Main Street gives the first 90 minutes free. Here is what each center publishes:

  • South Coast Plaza: valet is $15 a car at three stations (near Seasons 52, near Chanel and Dior, and near South Coast Drive). The center notes that valet hours may vary during the holidays.
  • Fashion Island: its parking page lists EV chargers in the Bloomingdale's lot and on the upper level of the garages near Neiman Marcus, at $1 an hour.
  • Pacific City: it offers parking validation for shoppers but does not publish how many hours. Ask at the store when you pay.
  • Main Street: the Main Promenade structure (enter on Olive or Walnut) is free for 90 minutes, then $1 per 20 minutes. Our Huntington Beach parking guide has all the downtown and beach lots.

None of the centers posts a Black Friday parking plan or shuttle for 2026. Signs in the lot win over this guide.

How do we avoid the worst Black Friday traffic?

Go early, or go late. Opening hour and the evening are calmer than the middle of the day, when the people who slept in all arrive at once.

  • Do the far center first. If you want The Outlets at Orange or Fashion Island, go at opening, and finish closer to home at Pacific City or Bella Terra in the afternoon.
  • South Coast Plaza is a 405 trip. It sits beside the freeway in Costa Mesa. If the 405 is backed up, surface streets via Brookhurst Street or Harbor Boulevard are often steadier. A map app on the day will know better than we do.
  • Rental car on the 405? The two left lanes on the 405 between the 73 in Costa Mesa and the 605 are the 405 Express Lanes, and they need a FasTrak transponder. Stay right of the double white lines and the 405 is free. Our toll roads guide explains the rest.
  • Thursday morning is a different problem. The Turkey Wobble runs at Pacific Coast Highway and Beach Boulevard on Thanksgiving morning, and the king tide that morning can flood PCH in Sunset Beach. See the Thanksgiving week guide.
  • Split the group. Not everyone wants to shop. The other half can take the beach, Bolsa Chica, or Knott's Merry Farm, which is open until 10 p.m. on Black Friday.

A simple Black Friday plan from Huntington Beach

  1. 6 a.m.: The Outlets at Orange opens. If outlets are the goal, be there for the first two hours.
  2. 10 a.m.: Head back toward the coast. Pacific City opens at 10. Bella Terra is on the way if it is open.
  3. Lunch and the beach: eat on Main Street or at Pacific City, then walk the sand before the 4:45 p.m. sunset.
  4. Evening: Fashion Island's snowfall tree runs every half hour from 5:30 to 8 p.m. if you still have energy. Our Christmas lights guide has the other free displays.
  5. Saturday: Small Business Saturday on Main Street in the morning, before the beach crowd.

Black Friday shopping FAQ

What time do malls open on Black Friday 2026 near Huntington Beach?

On Friday, November 27, 2026, The Outlets at Orange opens at 6 a.m. (to 10 p.m.), Fashion Island at 8 a.m. (to 8 p.m.), South Coast Plaza at 9 a.m. (to 9 p.m.) and Pacific City at 10 a.m. (to 9 p.m.). Citadel Outlets opens at 8 p.m. on Thanksgiving and stays open until 11 p.m. on Friday. Bella Terra had not posted 2026 holiday hours on November 21.

Are malls near Huntington Beach open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

Mostly no. South Coast Plaza, Fashion Island, The Outlets at Orange and Pacific City all list Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026 as closed. Fashion Island says select restaurants and stores may open. Citadel Outlets is the exception: it opens at 8 p.m. on Thanksgiving and runs overnight into Black Friday.

Is Bella Terra open on Black Friday 2026?

Probably, but its 2026 holiday hours were not posted on November 21, 2026. The holiday sheet on its website is from 2025, when Bella Terra was closed on Thanksgiving and open 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday. Its regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday to Saturday and 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday.

When is Small Business Saturday 2026?

Saturday, November 28, 2026, according to the U.S. Small Business Administration. Downtown Huntington Beach had not posted a Small Business Saturday event on November 21, but the independent shops on Main Street between Pacific Coast Highway and Orange Avenue are open, each on its own hours.

Where should I park for Black Friday shopping in Huntington Beach?

Downtown, use the Main Promenade structure at 200 Main Street: the first 90 minutes are free, then $1 per 20 minutes. Pacific City offers validation for shoppers but does not publish how many hours. At South Coast Plaza, valet is $15 a car. Arrive before noon for the easiest parking.
