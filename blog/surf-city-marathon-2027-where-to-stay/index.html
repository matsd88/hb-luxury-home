Surf City Marathon 2027: Race Weekend Guide and Where to Stay

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated October 25, 2026

Short answer: the 2027 Surf City Marathon is on Sunday, February 7, 2027. The marathon starts at 6:30 a.m., the half at 7:15 a.m. and the 5K at 11:00 a.m., all at Pacific Coast Highway and Huntington Street, next to the Pier. You must pick up your bib at the expo on Friday, February 5 (noon to 6 p.m.) or Saturday, February 6 (10 a.m. to 4 p.m.) unless you paid for race-morning pickup. So most runners arrive Friday and stay Friday and Saturday night. If you are running the full marathon, book Sunday night too: the course limit is 6.5 hours, and a typical 11 a.m. checkout lands in the middle of your race.

Everything below comes from the race's own pages (schedule, expo, parking and travel), checked October 25, 2026. The race updates these pages in the weeks before the event, and some of them still show 2026 dates in places, so check them again race week.

Sunrise on February 7, 2027 is about 6:44 a.m. at the Pier (worked out with NOAA's solar equations), so the marathon starts in twilight and you will be walking to the start in the dark. Bring a throwaway layer.

Which nights to book

  • Half marathon or 5K: Friday and Saturday nights. Pick up your bib Friday afternoon or Saturday, race Sunday morning, and you can be showered and out by a late-morning checkout. With a 7:15 a.m. start and a 4.5-hour limit, the last half finishers come in around 11:45 a.m., so if you expect to take more than about three hours, add Sunday night or ask about checkout before you book.
  • Full marathon: Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights. A 6:30 a.m. start with a 6.5-hour limit means the last finishers come in around 1 p.m., and gear check stays open until 1 p.m. Even a four-hour runner is not back, showered and packed by 11 a.m. A Monday checkout also saves you from a long drive or flight on tired legs.
  • Beach Mile on Saturday plus a Sunday race: the race has challenge medals for running more than one event (see its Challenges pages). Arrive Friday so you can pick up everything at once before the Beach Mile pickup closes at 10:30 a.m. Saturday.
  • Flying in: John Wayne (SNA) and Long Beach (LGB) are the close airports. The airport guide compares them with LAX.

Packet pickup: what to bring, and pickup for a group

Pickup is at the expo in the Huntington Beach Parking Lots, 21291 Pacific Coast Highway, and it is free and open to the public. Bring a photo ID and the QR code confirmation email the race sends on the Wednesday of race week. Parking in the City lot next to the expo is free on Friday and Saturday: enter at Pacific Coast Highway and Beach Boulevard and head north into the lots. The State Beach lots on the south side of Beach Boulevard are paid on those days.

For a group, one person can collect for others. The race allows a proxy to pick up for up to 3 people, as long as each runner signed the digital waiver at registration. Send the proxy with a copy of each runner's driver's license (a photo on a phone is fine) and their QR code email. If you cannot get there Friday or Saturday at all, race-morning pickup is a paid add-on for the marathon, half and 5K, bought through your registration.

Getting to the start on race morning

The race recommends arriving 45 to 60 minutes before your start. For the marathon, that means being at the start area by about 5:30 to 5:45 a.m. Parts of Pacific Coast Highway are closed for the course (it runs north past Bolsa Chica, and the marathon also loops through Central Park), so plan on parking away from PCH and riding a shuttle.

  • Free parking with a shuttle (Sunday): the Civic Center lot (1900 Main Street) and the Edison High School lot (21400 Magnolia Street). Both are away from closed streets, and shuttles drop off one to two blocks from the start. Shuttles run on a rolling basis from 5 a.m. to 1 p.m.; the last shuttle for the 5K leaves at 10 a.m.
  • Free beach lots: the State Beach lots south of Beach Boulevard are included in the free program on Sunday, with shuttle stops near Newland and between Magnolia and Brookhurst. The race puts the walk at about half a mile from the Beach Boulevard entrance and 2.25 miles from Brookhurst.
  • Paid and closest: the public lot at Beach Boulevard and PCH (entrance on Beach) is $20 all day for runners who enter before noon and opens at 4:30 a.m. The City Beach lot north of Beach and PCH is $20 on entry and fills early. Pacific City (21010 PCH, entrance on Pacific View Avenue) is a block from the start, at the operator's hourly rate.
  • Getting dropped off or taking a rideshare: use the drop-off area at Beach Boulevard and Atlanta Avenue (21229 Beach Blvd), then walk about three blocks to PCH and up to the start.
  • Do not park at the Beach Promenade shopping center (Beach and Atlanta) or the Newland shopping center (Beach between Adams and Yorktown). The race says cars there may be towed.

Our parking guide covers the normal rates at these lots on non-race days, which is useful for the expo and for dinner downtown on Saturday.

Where to stay: hotel by the start or a house for the group

We should say plainly that we own and host one of the options below. Here is how they compare for this race.

The race's host hotel is the Waterfront Beach Resort (21100 Pacific Coast Highway), steps from the start. The race lists marathon-weekend rooms from $288 a night before tax, as a 20% discount off the hotel's online rates. If you are one or two runners, want to roll out of bed and walk to the start, and do not need a kitchen, this is the easiest race morning in town, and nothing else beats it on that.

A house makes more sense when you are a group: a running club, a couple of families, or friends doing different distances. You split one booking, cook your own carbs on Saturday night, and do not need a separate room for each pair. The trade-off is that you drive or rideshare to the start instead of walking.

Our house, honestly

Luxury HB Home is a 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath house in Huntington Beach 92649 that sleeps 7. It is about 10 to 15 minutes off-peak by car from the Pier area (drive times), so on race morning you would drive to the Civic Center or Edison High lot and take the shuttle, or get dropped off at Beach and Atlanta. Allow extra time; race-morning detours are the unknown.

  • Saturday night: a full kitchen with quartz counters and dining for the group, so the pre-race pasta is on your schedule, not a restaurant's.
  • Race morning: 2.5 bathrooms for 7 people. Stagger alarms by start time: marathoners first, then the half, and 5K runners can sleep in.

Who it is not for: a solo runner who wants to walk to the start line, or anyone who would rather not drive at 5 a.m. Book the host hotel or something in the Pacific City and downtown area instead. And if your group is larger than 7, you will need a second place.

For the people cheering

Both long courses head north on PCH from the start, past the Pier, and come back past it before the finish, so the Pier and the stretch of PCH near the start are the easiest places to see a runner twice. The marathon also goes inland through Huntington Central Park and runs about eight miles on the beachfront path, which stays open to the public (the race warns it is only 8 feet wide in places). Supporters with the day free can fill it with the Bolsa Chica wetlands, which the course passes, or the ideas in things to do in Huntington Beach. February is also gray whale season, if you are staying Monday.

What we cannot tell you

The race has not posted the 2027 road-closure map, course maps sometimes change, and the hotel rate, shuttle times and parking details above are what the race listed on October 25, 2026. The race publishes a race weekend guide before the event (the 2026 version is still up and shows the format), and its pages are the final word.

Surf City Marathon FAQ

When is the 2027 Surf City Marathon?

Sunday, February 7, 2027. The marathon starts at 6:30 a.m., the half marathon at 7:15 a.m. and the 5K at 11 a.m., at Pacific Coast Highway and Huntington Street. The Beach Mile is Saturday, February 6 at 11 a.m.

When and where is packet pickup for Surf City Marathon 2027?

At the expo in the Huntington Beach Parking Lots, 21291 Pacific Coast Highway: Friday, February 5 from noon to 6 p.m. and Saturday, February 6 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Bring photo ID and the QR code email sent the Wednesday of race week. Race-morning pickup is a paid add-on.

Can someone else pick up my Surf City Marathon bib?

Yes. A proxy can pick up for up to 3 people if each runner signed the waiver at registration. Send a copy of each runner's driver's license and their QR code confirmation email.

Where do I park for the Surf City Marathon?

Free parking with shuttles is at the Civic Center lot (1900 Main Street), Edison High School (21400 Magnolia Street) and the State Beach lots south of Beach Boulevard. Shuttles run from 5 a.m. to 1 p.m. Paid lots near the start include the Beach Boulevard and PCH public lot ($20 for runners, opens 4:30 a.m.) and Pacific City.

What is the Surf City Marathon time limit?

6.5 hours for the marathon (14:53 per mile), 4.5 hours for the half (20:36 per mile) and 1.5 hours for the 5K. The course reopens on a rolling basis behind the pace.
