New Year's Eve in Huntington Beach 2026: Where to Be at Midnight (Not on the Sand)

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 4, 2026

Short answer: Huntington Beach has no public New Year's Eve fireworks, and you can't count down on the sand. The city beach closes at 10 p.m., the state beaches at 10 p.m. (gates at 9), and the Pier at midnight. Fireworks are legal in the city only on July 4, noon to 10 p.m., so setting off your own on December 31 is illegal, on the beach or anywhere else. For fireworks at midnight, the nearest show is the Queen Mary in Long Beach, about 13 miles and 30 minutes from north Huntington Beach: a 15-minute show over the harbor at midnight, with party tickets from $175. Disneyland, about 27 minutes away, had midnight fireworks last year, but it hasn't posted its 2026 schedule yet. Or keep it simple: watch the 4:54 p.m. sunset at the Pier, have a fire until 10, and ring in 2027 back at the house. On New Year's Day, the Surf City Splash ocean dip has been held at noon by the Pier for 26 years.

This guide is for families and groups staying in Huntington Beach over New Year's who are asking "where do we go at midnight?" and "can we just be on the beach?" New Year's Eve 2026 falls on a Thursday, and New Year's Day 2027 on a Friday. Rules come from the City of Huntington Beach's beach information page (Municipal Code Chapter 13.08) and its fireworks page. Event details come from the organizers' own pages, all checked November 4, 2026. Drive times are OpenStreetMap routing from north Huntington Beach (92649) with no traffic, and the sunset time is calculated from NOAA's solar formulas for the Pier.

The city hasn't announced any New Year's Eve exception to these hours. Expect the 10 p.m. curfew to apply as on any other night.

Option 1: Sunset and a fire, then midnight at the house

This is the plan I'd pick for a group with kids or grandparents. On December 31 the sun sets at about 4:54 p.m., so get to the Pier by 4:15, walk out for the sunset, then head to a fire pit. The city has 127 fire pits between First Street and Beach Boulevard, and on a holiday night the free first-come rings go early. The bonfire and fire pit guide explains the rings you can reserve just south of the Pier, what to bring, and the rules. Pack warm layers: December evenings by the water drop into the 50s and lower (see the November and December weather guide).

Be off the sand by 10, and you're home with two hours to spare. A good trick for younger kids: the ball in Times Square drops at midnight Eastern, which is 9 p.m. Pacific. Watch it live, call that midnight, and the adults can stay up for the real one.

Option 2: The Queen Mary's midnight fireworks in Long Beach

The closest public midnight fireworks to Huntington Beach are at the Queen Mary, the retired ocean liner docked in Long Beach. It's about 13.2 miles and 29 minutes from north Huntington Beach with no traffic. The ship's New Year's Eve page lists a Glitz & Glamour Masquerade to ring in 2027, ending with a 15-minute fireworks show over Long Beach Harbor at midnight.

  • Tickets: general admission $175 in advance or $195 on the day. VIP is $295 (first release, limited).
  • Dress: your best outfit and a masquerade mask.
  • Age: hotel packages for the night are 21+. The page doesn't state an age limit for the party ticket itself, so ask before you buy if anyone under 21 is coming.
  • No ticket? The fireworks go up over the harbor, so you can see them from the downtown Long Beach waterfront across the water. Get there early and plan for crowds and paid parking.

Don't drive back after the party. Book a rideshare home, or have a sober driver. Prices surge right after midnight, so waiting 30 to 45 minutes often helps.

Option 3: Disneyland on New Year's Eve

Disneyland is about 15 miles and 27 minutes from the house. On New Year's Eve 2025, Disneyland Park put on its New Year's Eve Fantasy in the Sky fireworks at midnight (DAPS Magic's report), and the area in front of the castle filled up before the countdown. As of November 4, Disneyland hasn't posted its hours or entertainment schedule for December 31, 2026. Check the Disneyland app or its official calendar before you buy tickets, because it's one of the busiest nights of the year. If you go, choose a viewing spot early, and expect the drive back to take a lot longer than 27 minutes. The kids and Disneyland guide covers day trips from the house.

Option 4: Dinner or a party downtown

Main Street and Pacific City restaurants and bars run their own New Year's Eve dinners and parties, most of them ticketed or by reservation, and they sell out. Decide by early December and book directly with the venue. Parking near the Pier is easier to deal with by rideshare that night; for daytime, see the Pier and Main Street parking guide.

New Year's Day 2027

  • Surf City Splash. Every January 1, hundreds of people run into the cold Pacific at the Pier. The 26th Splash on January 1, 2026, ran from 10:15 a.m., with the dip at noon at Pier Plaza (325 Pacific Coast Highway) and a vendor village. It's organized by the Huntington Beach International Surfing Museum, which hadn't posted 2027 details as of November 4. Watching is free.
  • The Rose Parade. Pasadena is about an hour north. The Rose Parade 2027 guide covers grandstand seats, the free curb (camping starts at noon on December 31) and the train from Long Beach. It also means a very early night on the 31st.
  • A slow morning. Beach walks start when the beach opens again in the morning; the sun rises at about 6:57 a.m., behind you if you're facing the ocean.

A simple New Year's plan from Huntington Beach

  1. Pick your midnight now: the house, the Queen Mary or Disneyland. Queen Mary tickets get more expensive on the day, and venue parties sell out.
  2. Book a 3-night stay around the 31st, for example Wednesday, December 30 to Saturday, January 2, so New Year's Day is a rest day.
  3. Be at the Pier by 4:15 p.m. on December 31 for the 4:54 sunset.
  4. Off the sand by 10 p.m. Leave the fireworks at home; they're illegal here on any night except July 4.
  5. Book rideshares for anyone going out, and plan the drive to Pasadena if you're doing the parade.

New Year's Eve FAQ

Are there fireworks in Huntington Beach on New Year's Eve?

No public show, and private fireworks are illegal. The city allows safe-and-sane fireworks only on July 4, from noon to 10 p.m. The nearest midnight fireworks are the Queen Mary's 15-minute show over Long Beach Harbor, about 13 miles away.

Can you be on the beach at midnight on New Year's Eve in Huntington Beach?

No. Huntington City Beach closes at 10 p.m., Huntington State Beach and Bolsa Chica State Beach close at 10 p.m. with gates closing at 9, and the Pier has a midnight curfew. The city has not announced any New Year's Eve exception.

Where can I see New Year's Eve fireworks near Huntington Beach?

The Queen Mary in Long Beach, about 30 minutes from north Huntington Beach, has a 15-minute fireworks show over the harbor at midnight as part of its masquerade party (general admission $175 in advance, $195 on the day). Disneyland had midnight fireworks on New Year's Eve 2025, but has not posted its December 31, 2026 schedule yet.

What time is sunset on New Year's Eve in Huntington Beach?

About 4:54 p.m. on December 31, 2026. Get to the Pier by 4:15 p.m. for the best light.

What is the Surf City Splash?

A New Year's Day dip into the ocean at the Huntington Beach Pier, run by the Huntington Beach International Surfing Museum. The 26th Splash on January 1, 2026, went in at noon at Pier Plaza. 2027 details had not been posted as of November 4, 2026.
