The Nutcracker and A Christmas Carol Near Huntington Beach, 2026: Dates, Tickets and Parking

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 22, 2026

Short answer: American Ballet Theatre's The Nutcracker runs December 10 to 24, 2026, at Segerstrom Hall in Costa Mesa, about 20 minutes from Huntington Beach. Tickets start at $34, and Segerstrom Center says early-bird prices go up on Thanksgiving Day, November 26. For something closer and cheaper, Ballet Etudes (December 5 to 13) and Ballet Repertory Theatre (December 11 to 24) dance it in Huntington Beach.

This guide is for a family or group staying in Huntington Beach in December who wants one holiday show: a grandparent who wants the big ballet, parents with a 5-year-old who will not sit for two hours, or teenagers who would rather see Scrooge. Every date, time and price below comes from the company's or venue's own website, read on November 22, 2026. Where something is not posted, we say so.

Segerstrom Center lists 18 performances from Thursday, December 10 to Thursday, December 24, 2026, in Segerstrom Hall, with Pacific Symphony playing Tchaikovsky's score live. The choreography is Alexei Ratmansky's, and the Center calls this its 11th season of the production.

Is there a shorter Nutcracker for young kids?

Yes. Pacific Symphony's The Nutcracker for Kids is on Saturday, December 5, 2026, at 10 a.m. and 11:30 a.m. in the Renée and Henry Segerstrom Concert Hall, 615 Town Center Drive, Costa Mesa. It is a narrated version with dancers from the ABT William J. Gillespie School.

Pacific Symphony's page recommends it for ages 5 and up, and every attendee needs a ticket. Lobby activities start at 9 a.m. for the 10 a.m. concert and at 12:15 p.m. for the 11:30 a.m. concert (after the show). They include carolers, an ornament craft, a coloring corner and an instrument petting zoo. A quiet zone on the third floor is there for kids who need a break. The page does not post the running time or ticket prices outside the checkout.

When is A Christmas Carol at South Coast Repertory in 2026?

South Coast Repertory's A Christmas Carol runs November 28 to December 27, 2026, on the Segerstrom Stage at 655 Town Center Drive, Costa Mesa, next door to Segerstrom Center. Previews run November 28 to December 3. It is the theater's long-running Jerry Patch adaptation, directed by Hisa Takakuwa.

From SCR's show page:

  • Length: about two hours, including one 15-minute intermission.
  • Ages: 6 and up. Children under 6 are not admitted, and everyone needs a ticket. The show uses smoke, haze and strobe effects.
  • Usual times: Saturdays at 2:30 and 7:30 p.m., Sundays at noon and 4 p.m., weeknights at 7 p.m., Fridays at 7:30 p.m. Christmas Eve has noon and 4 p.m. shows, there is no show on Christmas Day, and the last show is Sunday, December 27 at noon. Some weeknights are dark, so check the calendar for your date.
  • Sensory-friendly performance: Tuesday, December 1 at 7 p.m.
  • ASL-interpreted performance: Saturday, December 5 at 2:30 p.m.
  • In the lobby: a hot chocolate bar at every performance ($15, in a souvenir mug); free Selfies with Santa from 6 to 6:50 p.m. on December 2, 9, 15, 16, 22 and 23; and free holiday jazz from 3 to 3:50 p.m. on Sundays December 6, 13 and 20.
  • Tickets: SCR says scr.org and its box office at (714) 708-5555 are the only authorized sellers. The show page does not list a price range; seat prices appear when you pick a date.

Which holiday show should we pick?

Pick by the youngest person in the group and how much you want to spend. Here is how we would choose:

  • Under 5: the free Bella Terra performance on November 29. Pacific Symphony recommends 5 and up, SCR does not admit under-6s, and Segerstrom Center does not admit babies in arms.
  • Ages 5 to 8: The Nutcracker for Kids on December 5, or a Ballet Etudes 2 p.m. matinee with the talk before the curtain.
  • The big night out: ABT at Segerstrom Hall. It is the only one with a professional company and a full live orchestra. Book before Thanksgiving Day for the early-bird price.
  • On a budget: Ballet Repertory Theatre at Golden West College, $25 to $30 a seat and close to the house.
  • Teenagers or a mixed-age group: A Christmas Carol at SCR. It is a play, not a ballet, so the story carries people who do not like dance.
  • Arriving after December 20: ABT runs to a Christmas Eve noon matinee, Ballet Repertory Theatre to December 24, and A Christmas Carol to December 27.

Where do we park at Segerstrom Center and South Coast Repertory?

Performance parking in the garages beside both theaters is $15. Pick the garage by the hall: Segerstrom Hall and South Coast Repertory are served by different garages.

  • For ABT's Nutcracker (Segerstrom Hall): the Center Tower Parking Structure, on Sunflower Avenue between Avenue of the Arts and Park Center Drive. It has EV chargers on the first and third floors and accessible spaces on every level. Card only, or prepay online.
  • For The Nutcracker for Kids (Concert Hall) and A Christmas Carol (SCR): the Plaza Tower Parking Structure, off Park Center Drive, behind the Concert Hall and SCR. SCR's parking page says to turn from Bristol Street onto Anton Boulevard, then left on Park Center Drive. Pay $15 on entry and keep the validated receipt for the exit.
  • Valet: $30 on Town Center Drive, in the circle in front of Segerstrom Hall, first come, first served.
  • Garages are third-party: both theaters say they do not run the garages, and rates can change.

Getting there from Huntington Beach: the campus is by the 405 at Bristol Street, across from South Coast Plaza. Allow about 20 minutes off-peak and more on a December weekday evening, when the 405 is slow. Stay out of the two left lanes, which are the 405 Express Lanes and need a FasTrak (our toll roads in a rental car guide explains). For the Huntington Beach shows, we did not find parking details on the Golden West College or Ballet Etudes pages, so arrive early and follow the signs.

How do we avoid overpaying for tickets?

Buy from the company or venue. Resale sites show up first in search results, and their prices can be far above face value.

  • Segerstrom Center's FAQ warns that resellers' service fees are "often more than 300% of face value". Its box office is in Segerstrom Hall, open Monday 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and Tuesday to Friday noon to 5 p.m., and until 30 minutes after the last show on performance days.
  • SCR sells A Christmas Carol only at scr.org and (714) 708-5555.
  • Ballet Repertory Theatre's tickets are sold through Golden West College's box office (gwctickets.com, (714) 895-8150). For Ballet Etudes, start from its own Nutcracker page.

The Nutcracker near Huntington Beach FAQ

When is The Nutcracker at Segerstrom Center in 2026?

American Ballet Theatre's The Nutcracker runs December 10 to 24, 2026, in Segerstrom Hall, 600 Town Center Drive, Costa Mesa, with Pacific Symphony playing the score. There are 18 performances. Evening shows start at 7 p.m. and matinees at 2 p.m., 12:30 p.m. or noon. The last show is a noon matinee on Christmas Eve.

How much are Nutcracker tickets at Segerstrom Center?

Segerstrom Center lists tickets starting at $34 for the 2026 run. On November 22 its page said early-bird prices go up on Thanksgiving Day, November 26, 2026. Every person needs a ticket regardless of age, and babies in arms are not admitted. Buy at scfta.org or the box office to avoid resale fees.

Is there a Nutcracker in Huntington Beach itself?

Yes. Ballet Etudes performs at the Huntington Beach High School theater on December 5, 6 and 13, 2026 ($45 orchestra, $35 balcony). Ballet Repertory Theatre performs at Golden West College on December 11 to 13, 18 to 20 and 22 to 24 ($30, or $25 for kids under 12 and seniors). Bella Terra has a free Ballet Etudes performance on Sunday, November 29 at 11 a.m.

When is A Christmas Carol at South Coast Repertory in 2026?

South Coast Repertory's A Christmas Carol runs November 28 to December 27, 2026, on the Segerstrom Stage at 655 Town Center Drive, Costa Mesa. Previews run through December 3. It lasts about two hours with one 15-minute intermission, and children under 6 are not admitted. Tickets are sold only at scr.org or (714) 708-5555.

Where do you park for Segerstrom Center and South Coast Repertory?

Performance parking in the garages next to both theaters is $15. For Segerstrom Hall, use the Center Tower structure on Sunflower Avenue. For the Concert Hall and South Coast Repertory, use the Plaza Tower structure off Park Center Drive. Valet in front of Segerstrom Hall is $30, first come, first served.

Make it a full evening

The theaters are a short drive from the best free light displays. After a matinee, our Christmas lights guide has the Snoopy House in Costa Mesa (December 11 to 18) and Fashion Island's snowfall tree, both a short drive from Segerstrom Center.
