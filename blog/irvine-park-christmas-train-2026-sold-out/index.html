Irvine Park Christmas Train 2026 Is Sold Out: What You Can Still Do

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 26, 2026

Short answer: Yes, the 2026 Christmas Train at Irvine Park Railroad is sold out, and the railroad does not release more seats or sell any at the gate. You can still walk into Santa's Village for free from November 27 to December 30 (closed December 24 and 25), and Train of Lights tickets for December 26 to 30 go on sale December 7 at 10 a.m.

This guide is for a family staying in Huntington Beach for the holidays who heard about the Santa train in Orange, went to book it over Thanksgiving weekend and found the "sold out" banner. It tells you what is still open to you at the park, what each part costs, and where else the kids can see Santa. Everything below is from Irvine Park Railroad's own event pages, read on November 26, 2026. The railroad says prices and details can change without notice.

Is the Irvine Park Christmas Train really sold out for 2026?

Yes. On November 26, 2026, the railroad's home page and its Christmas Train page both showed the notice "Christmas Train tickets are sold out." Tickets went on sale online on Monday, November 2, 2026, at 10 a.m.

  • No re-release: the railroad says that when a date and time sells out, "there will be no additional tickets for sale" for it.
  • No walk-ups: "Tickets cannot be purchased in person or over the phone."
  • No waitlist: the page does not mention one.

We could not see date-by-date availability ourselves, so if you are set on it, check the ticket page once more. We would not plan a day around a seat opening up.

Can we see Santa at Irvine Park without a train ticket?

No. The railroad says "Christmas Train tickets must be purchased in advance to visit Santa and to take a photo with him." Santa is at the North Pole stop on the train route, not in Santa's Village.

That is the part that catches families out: Santa's Village is free to walk into, but walking in does not get you to Santa. If seeing Santa is the goal this year, skip to where else to see Santa below.

What does Santa's Village cost, and when is it open?

Santa's Village is free to enter with no reservation. You pay only for the activities you choose, with tickets at $8 each or 15 for $100. Activity tickets cannot be used on either train.

What the activity tickets buy, per the Santa's Village page:

  • 1 ticket: moon bounce, cookie decorating, craft corner, Rudolph Racers (ages 5 to 7), and the small prize at carnival games, Blizzard Ball or Bank-a-ball
  • 2 tickets: mini digger (ages 5 and up), ice fishing, and the large prize at the games
  • Free: the picture scenes
  • Food: sold separately, prices vary

For two kids doing cookie decorating, the moon bounce and the mini digger each, that is 8 tickets, or $64. On a weekday before December 14 the village does not open until 4 p.m., so a weekday visit is an evening outing.

What is the Train of Lights, and when do tickets go on sale?

The Train of Lights is the after-Christmas ride: a 1-mile loop through the park's lights, December 26 to 30, 2026, from 5 to 9 p.m., with no stops and no Santa. Tickets go on sale online only on Monday, December 7, 2026, at 10 a.m., and they sell out by time slot.

Children under 24 months ride free on both and do not need a ticket. The Train of Lights page lists the online fee two different ways, $10 per order in one answer and $5 per ticket in another, so expect one or the other at checkout. You can buy up to 8 tickets per order, and tickets are non-refundable and cannot be moved to another date or time.

If you are staying through New Year's, set an alarm for 10 a.m. on December 7. The later slots are the cheapest, and 8 p.m. for a family of six is $66 before fees.

How much is parking, and how far is it from Huntington Beach?

Irvine Regional Park charges a vehicle entrance fee of $3 on weekdays, $5 on weekends and $7 on holidays, and parking is free after 6 p.m., according to the railroad's event pages. The pages do not say which days count as holidays.

The park is at 1 Irvine Park Road, Orange, CA 92869, about 22 miles from north Huntington Beach. That is roughly 35 minutes in light traffic. A weekday visit starts at 4 p.m., right in rush hour, so leave extra time; arriving after 6 p.m. also saves the entrance fee.

Where else can the kids see Santa near Huntington Beach?

Closer to the beach, Santa is at Pacific City and Bella Terra in Huntington Beach, and at South Coast Plaza and Fashion Island, all through December 24. Visits are free at all four; you pay for the photo package.

Irvine Park Christmas Train FAQ

Are Irvine Park Railroad Christmas Train tickets sold out for 2026?

Yes. On November 26, 2026, the Irvine Park Railroad website showed the notice "Christmas Train tickets are sold out." Tickets went on sale online on November 2. The railroad says sold-out time slots are not re-released and tickets cannot be bought in person or by phone.

Can you see Santa at Irvine Park without a Christmas Train ticket?

No. The railroad says Christmas Train tickets must be bought in advance to visit Santa and take a photo with him. Santa's Village next to the station is free to enter without a ticket, but Santa himself is reached by the train at the North Pole stop.

Is Santa's Village at Irvine Park free?

Yes. Santa's Village is free to enter with no reservation, November 27 to December 30, 2026, closed December 24 and 25. Activities such as cookie decorating, the moon bounce and the mini digger cost 1 or 2 activity tickets at $8 each, or 15 tickets for $100. The park charges a vehicle entrance fee before 6 p.m.

When do Irvine Park Train of Lights tickets go on sale in 2026?

The railroad says Train of Lights tickets go on sale online on December 7, 2026, at 10 a.m. The ride runs December 26 to 30 from 5 to 9 p.m. and costs $11 to $17 depending on the departure hour. Santa is not there on those dates.

How far is Irvine Park Railroad from Huntington Beach?

About 22 miles. Irvine Park Railroad is in Irvine Regional Park, 1 Irvine Park Road, Orange, CA 92869, roughly 35 minutes from north Huntington Beach in light traffic and longer at weekday rush hour, which is when the 4 p.m. weekday opening falls.
