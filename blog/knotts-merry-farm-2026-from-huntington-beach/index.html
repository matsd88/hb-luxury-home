Knott's Merry Farm 2026: Hours by Date, Show Times and the Best Night to Go from Huntington Beach

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 19, 2026

Short answer: Knott's Merry Farm runs Friday, November 20, 2026 through Sunday, January 3, 2027 at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park. It is included with a regular park ticket (from $65 online, plus parking from $40). The park is open every day of the run except Christmas Day. The catch is the hours: on most weekdays from November 30 to December 10 the park closes at 6 p.m., and on those days the evening shows don't run. For the full Christmas program, pick a night the park is open until 10 p.m., arrive by 2 p.m., and stay for the 5:15 p.m. tree lighting, Snow and Glow, and the 7:30 ice show. From Huntington Beach it is a straight drive up Beach Boulevard, about 14 miles from the Pier.

This guide is for families staying in Huntington Beach over Thanksgiving week or in December who want one day at Knott's for Christmas and need to know which day to pick. Hours and show times come from the Knott's park calendar, which we read for every date of the season on November 19, 2026. What is included and what costs extra is from the Knott's Merry Farm page. Knott's says show times can change without notice, so check the Six Flags app on the morning you go.

The Christmas Crafts Village in Ghost Town has local artisans selling handmade gifts, with chainsaw carving and glass-blowing demonstrations. Knott's says some of its activities cost extra. Mrs. Claus' Kitchen, where kids decorate a cupcake to take home, opens on select days for a small fee. The Merry Farm page says Santa photos are sold on site.

The ice show is the one that fills. We could not find a reservation system for it on the Knott's site, so plan to get in line 20 to 30 minutes before the show you want. On a long day, the 3:30 show leaves the whole evening free for the tree lighting and the outdoor shows.

Which day to pick

  • Best for the full program: any 10 p.m. night. Arrive by 2 p.m., ride while it's light, catch the 3:30 ice show, then stay for the 5:15 tree lighting, Snow and Glow at 5:30 and Home for the Holidays at 6:30. The sun sets around 4:45 p.m. in late November and December, so the lights are on by the tree lighting.
  • Thanksgiving week (Nov 20 to 28) has 10 p.m. closings every night, which makes it the easiest stretch to plan around if you're in Huntington Beach for the holiday. The park is open on Thanksgiving Day itself, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., with the full show schedule. It is a school-holiday week, so expect crowds. That is our judgment, not a published figure.
  • Skip if you want the shows: Monday to Thursday, November 30 to December 3, and December 7 to 10. The park closes at 6 p.m., the ice show isn't scheduled on the posted days, and you get only the tree lighting and one Snow and Glow.
  • Christmas week: open 10 to 10 from December 18 to 23, 10 to 7 on Christmas Eve, closed Christmas Day, then 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. from December 26. Christmas week and the days after are among the busiest of the year at Southern California parks. Our Christmas Day guide covers what is open on the 25th instead.
  • New Year's Eve runs from 9 a.m. to 1 a.m. The Merry Farm page does not list a separate New Year's event, so check the app for that night's program. For a beach New Year's, see our New Year's Eve in Huntington Beach guide.

Tickets and parking

  • Daily ticket: from $65 per person, online only, valid on select dates; the price changes by date. Knott's Merry Farm is included. See Knott's daily tickets.
  • Season pass: the 2027 Season Pass was listed at $120 paid in full (or $30 down and $9 a month), with seasonal event access and no blockout dates. If you would go twice, compare it with two daily tickets. Check what it covers before you buy.
  • Parking: daily parking starts at $40 and can be bought online. Knott's says it is not valid in the metered, preferred or hotel lots. See Knott's parking.
  • What's extra: food and drink, merchandise, Santa photos, some Crafts Village activities and the cupcake decorating. The Merry Farm page lists holiday food, hot cider and gourmet hot chocolate, and a Cranberry Bacon Jam Cheeseburger.

A rough budget for a family of four at the starting price: about $260 in tickets plus $40 parking, before food. Holiday dates may price higher.

Getting there from Huntington Beach

Knott's is at 8039 Beach Boulevard in Buena Park. Beach Boulevard is the same road that ends at Pacific Coast Highway by the Huntington Beach Pier, so the route is one long road north.

  • Drive: about 14 miles from the Pier, roughly 25 minutes with light traffic. Beach Boulevard has a lot of traffic lights, and on weekday afternoons it is slow. From our house in 92649 it is about 20 to 25 minutes off-peak. There are no toll roads on the way.
  • Leave by: 1:00 to 1:30 p.m. if you want to be inside by 2 and see the 3:30 ice show.
  • Bus: OCTA Route 29 runs along Beach Boulevard between Huntington Beach and Knott's. It is slow and makes many stops. Check the Route 29 timetable (effective November 8, 2026) for the last bus back before you plan a 10 p.m. night on it.
  • Rideshare avoids the $40 parking. At closing time a lot of people want a car at once, so set your pickup a little away from the main gate.
  • Dress warm. Buena Park is inland and cools off quickly after dark. A December night at Knott's is colder than the afternoon on the sand. Our November and December weather guide has the coastal numbers.

A plan that works: one 10 p.m. night

  1. 1:15 p.m. Leave Huntington Beach, straight up Beach Boulevard.
  2. 2:00. Park and go in. Ride the big rides while the lines are shorter.
  3. 3:00. Line up for the 3:30 Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies.
  4. 4:30. Visit Santa at the Wilderness Dance Hall, or walk the Christmas Crafts Village.
  5. 5:15. Tree lighting in Calico Park.
  6. 5:30. Snow and Glow in Ghost Town.
  7. 6:30. Home for the Holidays at the Calico Mine Stage. Dinner after.
  8. 8:00 to 9:30. A last ride or two with the lights on. Leave before closing to beat the parking-lot rush, and you're back in Huntington Beach by about 10.

With small children, do the same plan but leave after the 6:30 show.

Knott's or Disneyland for Christmas?

Both are about 25 minutes from Huntington Beach. Knott's is cheaper, smaller and easier to do in an afternoon and evening. Snoopy and the Peanuts gang suit preschoolers well. Disneyland has the bigger holiday spectacle and higher prices. Our kids and Disneyland guide covers that day. Many families staying a week do one of each.

Knott's Merry Farm FAQ

When is Knott's Merry Farm 2026?

Knott's Merry Farm runs Friday, November 20, 2026 through Sunday, January 3, 2027 at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park. The park's calendar shows it open every day of that run except Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, when the park is closed.

What are Knott's Merry Farm hours?

They change by date. On the park's calendar as of November 19, 2026: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. from November 20 to 28; 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on most weekdays from November 30 to December 10; 10 to 10 on December 5, 11, 12 and 18 to 23; 10 to 8 on December 6 and 13 to 17; 10 to 7 on Christmas Eve; 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. from December 26 to 30; 9 a.m. to 1 a.m. on New Year's Eve; and 10 to 10 from January 1 to 3.

Is Knott's Merry Farm included with a regular ticket?

Yes. Knott's says live shows, light displays, select activities and most rides are included with park admission. Food, drinks, merchandise, Santa photos, some Christmas Crafts Village activities and cupcake decorating at Mrs. Claus' Kitchen cost extra. Daily tickets start at $65 online and parking starts at $40.

What time is the tree lighting at Knott's Merry Farm?

The 'Tis the Season Tree Lighting Ceremony is at 5:15 p.m. in Calico Park on every date the park has published so far (through December 6, 2026), including the nights the park closes at 6 p.m.

What time is the Snoopy ice show at Knott's?

Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies plays in the Walter Knott Theater, usually at 3:30, 5:30 and 7:30 p.m., with an extra 1:30 p.m. show on weekends and the day after Thanksgiving. It is not scheduled on Monday, November 23, or on Monday to Thursday, November 30 to December 3. Later dates were not posted yet on November 19.

What is the best night to go to Knott's Merry Farm?

Pick a date the park is open until 10 p.m., arrive by 2 p.m. and stay into the evening. That gets you the ice show, the 5:15 tree lighting, Snow and Glow and Home for the Holidays in one visit. Avoid the 6 p.m. closing nights from November 30 to December 10 if the evening shows matter to you.

Is Knott's open on Christmas Day 2026?

No. The park calendar lists Knott's Berry Farm as closed on Friday, December 25, 2026. It is open 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Christmas Eve and reopens at 9 a.m. on December 26.

How far is Knott's Berry Farm from Huntington Beach?

Knott's is at 8039 Beach Boulevard in Buena Park, on the same road that ends at the Huntington Beach Pier. It is about 14 miles straight up Beach Boulevard from the Pier, roughly 25 minutes with light traffic, and about 20 to 25 minutes from our house in 92649 off-peak.
