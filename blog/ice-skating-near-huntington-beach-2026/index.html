Ice Skating Near Huntington Beach: Irvine Spectrum, Winter Fest OC and Indoor Rinks (Holidays 2026)

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 2, 2026

Short answer: Huntington Beach itself has no ice rink this season. Paséa Hotel, which used to put a rink on its oceanfront lawn, lists Holiday Igloos instead for 2026. The closest ice is Ice Realm Westminster, an indoor rink about 10 minutes from north Huntington Beach ($20 admission, $8 skates). For the outdoor, under-the-palm-trees version, go to the Irvine Spectrum Center rink, open November 6, 2026 to January 3, 2027 ($27 with skates, about 30 minutes away), or Winter Fest OC at the OC Fair & Event Center in Costa Mesa (select dates November 27 to January 3, about 20 minutes away). Both outdoor rinks close on Thanksgiving or Christmas Day, so check the holiday hours below before you promise the kids.

This guide is for families and groups spending Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's on the coast who want a skating night that feels like winter, and who need to know which rink, which session, what it costs and whether it's open on the day they're free. Prices, hours and dates come from each rink's own website, checked November 2, 2026: Irvine Spectrum Center, Winter Fest OC, Ice Realm Westminster, Great Park Ice and Paséa Hotel & Spa. Distances and no-traffic drive times are from OpenStreetMap routing from north Huntington Beach (92649).

Irvine Spectrum Center: the outdoor holiday rink

This is the one most people picture: an open-air rink in SunWheel Court, next to the center's new Spectrum SunWheel, with lit palm trees all around. The center's event page lists it open November 6, 2026 through January 3, 2027. Address: 670 Spectrum Center Drive, Irvine, CA 92618.

  • Sessions are 90 minutes: 2:00, 4:00, 6:00 and 8:00 p.m. Sunday to Thursday, plus a 10:00 p.m. session on Friday and Saturday.
  • Holiday hours: closed Thanksgiving Day (November 26) and Christmas Day (December 25). On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve the last session is 6:00 p.m. New Year's Day runs 2:00 to 9:30 p.m.
  • Tickets: $27 per person including skate rental, or $24 with your own skates. Seniors 62+ with ID pay $24 with rental; military with ID pay $24 with rental for up to 4 people. Tickets are sold at the rink booth only, not at Guest Services.
  • "Jump the Line": $45 per person, online only, with a separate entrance. A limited number are sold per session. On a December weekend night it can be worth it for a group that has already driven half an hour.
  • Extras: helmets $8 (recommended), socks $8, lockers $0.75.
  • Special nights: live DJ "Glide & Groove" sessions on Friday and Saturday at 6, 8 and 10 p.m.; Themed Thursdays at the 6 and 8 p.m. sessions, with $5 off if you dress for the theme (Ugly Sweater Night is December 17); and Skate with Santa on Saturday, December 5, 10 to 11:30 a.m., ticketed separately.
  • Weather: the rink may close in bad weather. If rain is in the forecast, call the box office at (714) 742-0160 before you leave.

Winter Fest OC: skating inside a holiday festival

Winter Fest OC returns to the OC Fair & Event Center, 88 Fair Drive, Costa Mesa, on select dates from November 27, 2026 to January 3, 2027. It is a full holiday festival, with a tubing hill, a walk-through light trail, a nightly tree lighting, a snow play zone and Santa until December 24, and this year skating is new: unlimited 45-minute sessions, subject to rink capacity, with skate rentals included.

  • How you pay: skating comes with select admission passes or as a separate add-on, and the add-on does not include festival admission. The site doesn't publish prices outside its ticketing page, and it says pricing changes with inventory, so compare on the tickets page before you buy.
  • For small kids: the smallest rental skate is a Child 8, which the festival says usually fits children around age 3. Skate assists rent for $15 at the rink office. Personal skates are not allowed.
  • Parking at the fairgrounds is $15, and the event is cashless.
  • Open dates are select dates only. Check the festival calendar for your night before you plan around it.

It's the best pick when skating is one part of the evening rather than the whole plan, and the closest of the outdoor options to the house.

Ice Realm Westminster: the closest ice, and a sure thing

Ice Realm Westminster (it used to be called The Rinks Westminster Ice) is at 13071 Springdale Street, Westminster, about 10 minutes from north Huntington Beach. It's indoors, so rain and a cold wind off the ocean don't matter, and it's the easiest rink for a first-time skater because there's no 30-minute drive and no outdoor weather to worry about.

  • Prices (public skating page): general admission $20, skate rental $8. Kids 7 and under are $15 including skates when they come with an adult. Seniors 55+ and military with ID are $18 including skates. Skate aids rent for $15.
  • Holiday sessions: the rink says it has added public skates around Thanksgiving and through December into January.
  • Before you go: every skater needs a customer account, so create one and add the kids before you buy. Pre-register online, especially on holidays and weekends, and arrive 30 to 45 minutes early. Your session starts at its scheduled time whether or not you're through the rental line yet.

Great Park Ice: a year-round rink in Irvine

Great Park Ice & FivePoint Arena, 888 Ridge Valley, Irvine runs public skating sessions every day. Admission is $21 for all ages, skate rental $8 and a skate aid $18. Its 2026 theme skates include a Holiday Skate on December 20. Buying online needs a free account, online sales close an hour before each session, and walk-ins are welcome. Tickets are non-refundable. Weekend and holiday sessions may stop for a 10-minute ice resurfacing. It's about the same drive as Irvine Spectrum, so choose it when you want indoor ice and are already in Irvine for the day.

What about Paséa's oceanfront rink?

For a few winters, Paséa Hotel & Spa at 21080 Pacific Coast Highway set up an ice rink on its lawn across from the beach, and older guides still list it. For 2026 the hotel's site lists Holiday Igloos instead, open November 27, 2026 to January 10, 2027: a private two-hour rental with firepit seating for up to 8 guests (12 in the large igloo), with valet parking at $15. On November 2, the hotel's site had no ice rink page, and the old rink address redirects to its general events page. If skating by the ocean is the whole reason for your trip, call the hotel at 855-622-2472 before you book around it. Otherwise, plan on one of the four rinks above.

A skating night from Huntington Beach

  1. Pick the rink by the night you have. Thanksgiving Day: Irvine Spectrum is closed, so try Ice Realm's added sessions. Christmas Day: Irvine Spectrum is closed too. Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve: Irvine's last session is 6 p.m.
  2. Buy or register ahead. Holiday sessions fill up at every rink. At Irvine Spectrum only the $45 "Jump the Line" ticket can be bought online, so arrive early for a regular ticket.
  3. Dress for it. Thick socks (or buy them for $8), gloves for little hands, and a warm layer. December lows in Huntington Beach average about 49°F, and it feels colder standing on ice outdoors.
  4. Book a 4 or 6 p.m. outdoor session if you want lights and darkness without a late night. Sunset is before 5 p.m. all December.
  5. Plan the rest of the evening: the Christmas guide has the Cruise of Lights and boat parade dates, so you can put skating on a different night.

Ice skating near Huntington Beach FAQ

Is there ice skating in Huntington Beach?

Not this season. Paséa Hotel & Spa, which once ran an oceanfront rink, lists Holiday Igloos for November 27, 2026 to January 10, 2027 instead. The closest rink is Ice Realm Westminster, an indoor rink at 13071 Springdale Street, about 10 minutes from north Huntington Beach.

When does the Irvine Spectrum ice rink open in 2026?

The Irvine Spectrum Center rink in SunWheel Court is open November 6, 2026 through January 3, 2027. Sessions are 90 minutes, starting at 2, 4, 6 and 8 p.m., plus 10 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. It is closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

How much is ice skating at Irvine Spectrum?

$27 per person including skate rental, or $24 with your own skates. Seniors 62+ and military with ID pay $24 including rental. "Jump the Line" tickets are $45, online only. Regular tickets are sold only at the rink booth.

Is there ice skating at Winter Fest OC in 2026?

Yes. New for 2026, Winter Fest OC at the OC Fair & Event Center in Costa Mesa has unlimited 45-minute skating sessions, with rentals included, as part of select passes or a separate add-on. The add-on doesn't include festival admission. The festival runs on select dates from November 27, 2026 to January 3, 2027, and parking is $15.

Can we ice skate on Christmas Day near Huntington Beach?

Not at Irvine Spectrum, which is closed on December 25. Indoor rinks such as Ice Realm Westminster and Great Park Ice post holiday sessions on their own schedules, so check their booking pages for Christmas Day before you plan on it.
