Thanksgiving Dinner To Go Near Huntington Beach (2026): Order Deadlines, Pickup and Prices

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 6, 2026

Short answer: If you're spending Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26, 2026) in a Huntington Beach rental and don't want to roast a whole turkey in a kitchen you've never used, order a cooked dinner from a grocery store and reheat it. Three are close to north Huntington Beach and have posted their 2026 rules. Whole Foods on Edinger (4 miles away) takes Thanksgiving catering orders until November 23, at least 48 hours before pickup, and lists a whole roast turkey meal for 8 at $169.99. Mother's Market asks you to reserve by November 21 at 11:59 p.m. and has pickup Sunday through Thursday of Thanksgiving week, with Thanksgiving Day pickup 8 a.m. to noon only. Gelson's in Newport Beach (about 16 miles) takes orders until November 24, with a roasted turkey dinner for up to 8 at $199. All of them come cold and need reheating. The easiest plan is to pick up on Wednesday and reheat on Thursday.

This guide is for a family or group that booked a house in Huntington Beach for Thanksgiving and is now wondering how dinner happens. You might be flying in on Tuesday or Wednesday, or you might just not want to spend your beach holiday basting. I checked each store's own ordering page on November 6, 2026. Prices and dates below are what those pages showed that day. Drive distances are OpenStreetMap routing from north Huntington Beach (92649) with no traffic.

Whole Foods Market (Bella Terra)

The closest full-service option is the Whole Foods at 7881 Edinger Avenue, at the Bella Terra center, open 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily according to its store page. The Whole Foods catering page says to order Thanksgiving catering by 11/23, and every catering order must be placed 48 hours before your pickup date and time. You choose the store and pickup slot when you order online, or you can call 1-844-936-2428.

The page listed these Thanksgiving meals on November 6:

  • Classic Whole Roast Turkey Meal, serves 8: $169.99 (a version for 4 is $99.99)
  • Classic Roast Turkey Breast Meal, serves 4: $89.99
  • Thanksgiving Extravaganza, serves 12: $539.99
  • Just-the-Sides packages, serve 4: Classic $59.99, Southern $69.99, Organic $79.99

Whole Foods says items "are delivered cold and may require reheating," and its page links to reheating instructions. Prices can differ at your store, so check the total before you pay.

Mother's Market (Sunset Beach and Huntington Beach)

Mother's Market is a Southern California natural-foods grocer with two stores close to the house: Sunset Beach at 16400 Pacific Coast Highway, Unit 112 (about 6 minutes away), and Huntington Beach at 19770 Beach Boulevard (about 14 minutes) (store list). Its Thanksgiving order FAQ gives the 2026 rules:

  • Reserve by November 21 at 11:59 p.m.
  • Pickup: Sunday, November 22 through Thursday, November 26, in three windows: 8 a.m.–noon, noon–4 p.m. or 4–8 p.m. On Thanksgiving Day, only the 8 a.m.–noon window is available.
  • No delivery. You pay at the register when you pick up. Turkey prices are set at checkout, by weight.
  • Changes: email mom@mothersmarket.com to change, cancel or add items.

On November 6 the order site said "Thanksgiving Reservations Open Soon!" Its categories include meals, uncooked turkeys, a fully cooked Organic Oven Roasted turkey, holiday sides, vegan dishes and desserts, but it hadn't posted items, prices or which stores take pickups yet. If you want to collect from the Sunset Beach store, confirm that when you reserve.

The stores list separate Thanksgiving Day hours: 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Sunset Beach and 6:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Huntington Beach. The page doesn't say which year those hours are for, so treat them as a guide.

Gelson's (Newport Beach)

Gelson's is the farthest of the three, at 1660 San Miguel Drive in Newport Beach. It has the clearest menu, though. Its Thanksgiving page says pickup runs November 20–29 and the last day to order is November 24. Every dinner is pickup only and comes refrigerated, ready to reheat.

  • Roasted Turkey Dinner: serves 2 (sliced breast) $65; up to 8 (12–15 lb turkey) $199; up to 12 (18–21 lb) $299
  • Spiral Cut Glazed Ham Dinner: serves 2 $65; up to 8 $170
  • Prime rib and brisket dinners for up to 8: $299 and $245
  • Impossible Meatloaf (plant-based), serves up to 4: $70

Since the pickup window runs to November 29, Gelson's also works if your group is doing Thanksgiving on Friday or Saturday.

Timing it around a rental check-in

A rental adds two problems a home cook doesn't have: you can't pick up dinner before you have the keys, and you're reheating in an oven you don't know yet.

  • Arriving Wednesday? Check-in at this house is 4 p.m. Book a Wednesday pickup after that time, such as Mother's 4–8 p.m. window or a late Whole Foods slot. If you land early, go to the beach first, not the store: cooked food shouldn't sit in the car while you wait to check in. The airport guide compares John Wayne, Long Beach and LAX for Thanksgiving week.
  • Arriving Thursday? Pick up on the way in and reheat that afternoon. Mother's Thursday pickup ends at noon, so this only works for a morning arrival.
  • Leave room in the fridge. A dinner for 8 comes in a lot of containers. Do the rest of the grocery shopping after pickup, not before.
  • Read the reheating sheet when you collect the order, not on Thursday afternoon. If the turkey needs a couple of hours in the oven, work back from dinner time and allow for an oven you haven't used.

Food safety is the same as at home. The CDC says to refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours (1 hour if it's above 90°F, as in a hot car). It also says to reheat leftovers to 165°F and check with a food thermometer. On a sunny November afternoon, a car parked in Orange County can heat up quickly, so go straight back to the house from the store. Bring a thermometer, or ask the host before you arrive whether the kitchen has one.

The half-and-half option

If part of your group loves cooking, split it. Buy a sides package (Whole Foods' Just-the-Sides, from $59.99 for 4) and roast only a turkey breast at the house. A whole bird needs most of the day in the oven. A breast is much quicker, and it leaves the oven free for the sides. At this house the BBQ island under the pergola gives you a second heat source for anything that goes on a grill, which takes pressure off the kitchen. The Thanksgiving week guide has the full cook-in plan, the Thanksgiving morning Turkey Wobble, and the rest of the week.

What I couldn't confirm

  • Restaurant takeout. Most Huntington Beach restaurants hadn't posted 2026 Thanksgiving to-go menus by November 6. Expect them in mid-November, and call the restaurant before you plan around one.
  • Mother's menu and prices, until reservations open.
  • Thanksgiving Day store hours for Whole Foods and Gelson's. Neither store page listed them, and grocery stores usually close early that day. Don't count on a Thursday-afternoon top-up shop.

Thanksgiving dinner to go FAQ

Where can I order a cooked Thanksgiving dinner near Huntington Beach?

Whole Foods at Bella Terra (7881 Edinger Ave) takes Thanksgiving catering orders until November 23, 2026, at least 48 hours before pickup. Its whole roast turkey meal for 8 is $169.99. Mother's Market (Sunset Beach and Huntington Beach stores) takes reservations until November 21 at 11:59 p.m. Gelson's in Newport Beach takes orders until November 24, with a roasted turkey dinner for up to 8 at $199.

Can I pick up a Thanksgiving dinner on Thanksgiving Day?

Sometimes. Mother's Market's only Thanksgiving Day pickup window is 8 a.m. to noon. Gelson's pickup runs from November 20 to 29. Whole Foods lets you pick a time when you order, at least 48 hours ahead. Picking up on Wednesday is safer, and you reheat on Thursday.

Are grocery store Thanksgiving dinners hot when you pick them up?

No. Whole Foods says its catering comes cold and may need reheating, and Gelson's says its dinners come refrigerated, ready to reheat. Keep the food cold and refrigerate it within 2 hours. Reheat leftovers to 165°F and check with a food thermometer.

Does Mother's Market deliver Thanksgiving orders?

No. Mother's Market says it does not deliver Thanksgiving orders. You pick up during your reserved window and pay at the register. Turkey prices are set at checkout.
