Sawdust Winter Fantasy 2026: Dates, Tickets and Parking from Huntington Beach

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 27, 2026

Short answer: Sawdust Winter Fantasy 2026 is open Friday to Sunday, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., on five weekends from November 20 to December 20 at 935 Laguna Canyon Road, Laguna Beach. Tickets are $12.50 for adults, $10.50 for seniors 65+ and $5.50 for ages 6 to 12. Park in Lot 16 for $5 and ride the free trolley; the drive from north Huntington Beach is about 24 miles.

This guide is for someone staying in Huntington Beach over the holidays who wants to buy gifts from the people who made them, and is checking on Thanksgiving weekend which days the Sawdust is open, what it costs for the group and where to park. Dates, hours, parking and rules below are from the Sawdust Art Festival's own site and its Plan Your Visit page, read on November 27, 2026. The festival says details can change, so check before you drive.

Which weekends is Sawdust Winter Fantasy open in 2026?

Five Friday-to-Sunday weekends, November 20 to December 20, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. each day. The Sawdust describes it as "Five three-day weekends, open Fri–Sun 11am–7pm." It is closed Monday through Thursday, and there are no dates after Sunday, December 20.

If you are buying gifts to ship home, go on an earlier weekend. The festival site does not post a shipping cutoff, so ask the artist before you buy something big.

How much are Sawdust Winter Fantasy tickets?

The Winter Fantasy page of the festival's ticket store lists the 2026 single-day ticket at $12.50 for adults, $10.50 for seniors (65+) and $5.50 for youth (6–12), plus a 2026 Winter Pass at $35.50, a season pass good for every Winter Fantasy day. Children under 5 are free with no ticket. You can buy online in advance or at the ticket booth when you arrive.

The ticket listings, read on November 27, 2026, also say tickets are non-refundable and ID is required at the gate, so bring it even if you bought online. One gap: free entry is for children under 5 and the youth ticket starts at 6, and the store does not say which ticket a 5-year-old needs, so ask at the booth.

For a full house of seven, say four adults, one grandparent aged 65 or older and two kids aged 6 to 12, single-day tickets come to $71.50, plus $5 for Lot 16 if you all fit in one car. The Winter Pass is a season pass for Winter Fantasy; at $35.50 it costs less than three adult single-day tickets ($37.50), so it pays off only if one person plans to come back on three different days.

How do we get there from Huntington Beach, and where do we park?

Drive about 24 miles to Laguna Canyon Road and park in Lot 16 for $5 a day, then take the free trolley. OpenStreetMap routing puts the drive from north Huntington Beach at roughly 35 minutes with no traffic. Its fastest line uses a short stretch of the 73, which is a toll road; stay on the 405 to Laguna Canyon Road (the 133) if you want to avoid tolls in a rental car (see toll roads in a rental car).

Parking options the Sawdust lists on its Plan Your Visit page:

  • Lot 16 (Act V), 1900 Laguna Canyon Road: $5 a day. It is about 0.6 miles up the canyon from the festival. The regular Canyon trolley runs from there 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., and on festival days an extra Canyon trolley runs 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • The Irvine lot off Laguna Canyon Road: free parking, served by the free Winter Express (Irvine 2) trolley from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on festival days. The Sawdust page does not give the lot's address; call the information booth at 949-494-3030 before relying on it.
  • Lots 10 and 11, 635 Laguna Canyon Road: a short walk from the gate, near downtown; the page lists no price.
  • Street meters on Frontage Road and Laguna Canyon Road: limited, and the meters take credit cards only.

On a Saturday in December we would go straight to Lot 16. The trolley drops you at the gate, and you are not circling the canyon for a meter.

What is there to do at Winter Fantasy?

Mostly, shop handmade art straight from the people who made it. The Sawdust's own list is "Browse art, take a class, talk to the artists, watch a live demo," with live music and entertainment on three stages during the festival season.

  • Artists' booths: the grounds are woodchip paths winding between the artists' booths; the artist directory lets you look up who works in glass, ceramics, jewelry, painting or wood before you go.
  • Classes and demos: the site mentions both but posts no Winter Fantasy class schedule on the pages we read, so check the art classes page or ask at the gate.
  • Food and drink: the Plan Your Visit page lists AhBa (burgers), Taco Loco, The Cove by Starfish, The Saloon and Annex (20 taps, wine, charcuterie), The Snack Shack, Espresso on the Go, and the Sand Bar and Waterfall Bar.

What we could not confirm: the festival pages we read on November 27 do not list Santa visits or a stage schedule for 2026, so do not plan the day around either. For Santa, use our photos with Santa near Huntington Beach guide instead.

What should we know before we go?

Leave the dog at the house, bring a jacket, and think twice about the stroller. These rules are all from the Sawdust's Plan Your Visit page:

  • No pets. Service animals only, leashed or harnessed, and approved by a supervisor at entry. Emotional support animals and dogs in training are not allowed.
  • Strollers are hard going. The paths are woodchips with slopes, there is no stroller parking, and the festival suggests a baby carrier or all-terrain wheels.
  • Wheelchairs: two beach-style wheelchairs can be reserved, availability limited, through the information booth at 949-494-3030.
  • Rain or shine. The festival stays open in bad weather.
  • No smoking of any kind, including e-cigarettes; the festival notes smoking anywhere in Laguna Beach is illegal.
  • Dress code: shirt and shoes required; bathing suits are not acceptable.

A simple Winter Fantasy afternoon from Huntington Beach

Go after lunch, shop for three hours, and finish with sunset on the sand at Main Beach. Sunset in early December is about 4:45 p.m. (see our November and December weather guide).

  1. 12:30 p.m. Leave Huntington Beach; park in Lot 16 around 1:15 p.m.
  2. 1:30 p.m. Trolley to the gate, buy or show tickets, and walk the booths. Note the artists you like and come back to them, because the grounds wind.
  3. 4:15 p.m. Walk down Laguna Canyon Road to Main Beach for sunset, roughly three-quarters of a mile, about 15 minutes on foot; the Laguna Beach day trip guide has the beach, food and trolley details.
  4. 5:15 p.m. Back to the festival for a drink and a last look before the 7 p.m. close, then trolley to the car.

Sawdust Winter Fantasy FAQ

When is Sawdust Winter Fantasy 2026?

Sawdust Winter Fantasy 2026 runs on five Friday-to-Sunday weekends from November 20 to December 20, 2026, open 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. each festival day, according to the Sawdust Art Festival website. The weekends still ahead after Thanksgiving are November 27-29, December 4-6, December 11-13 and December 18-20. It is closed Monday to Thursday.

How much are Sawdust Winter Fantasy tickets?

The festival's online ticket store lists the 2026 Winter Fantasy single-day ticket at $12.50 for adults, $10.50 for seniors 65 and over and $5.50 for youth 6 to 12, plus a 2026 Winter Pass (season pass) at $35.50. Children under 5 get in free with no ticket. Tickets are non-refundable, ID is required at the gate, and you can also buy at the ticket booth on arrival.

Where do you park for Sawdust Winter Fantasy?

Lot 16 at 1900 Laguna Canyon Road is $5 a day, with a free Canyon trolley to the festival. The Sawdust also lists free parking at an Irvine lot off Laguna Canyon Road with the free Winter Express trolley, 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on festival days, plus metered street parking (credit card only) and Lots 10 and 11 at 635 Laguna Canyon Road.

Can you bring a dog or a stroller to Sawdust Winter Fantasy?

No pets are allowed on the grounds; only service animals are permitted, leashed or harnessed, and approved by a supervisor at entry. Strollers are allowed but the festival warns they are tricky on the woodchip paths and slopes, there is no stroller parking, and it suggests a baby carrier. Two beach-style wheelchairs can be reserved by calling 949-494-3030.

How far is Sawdust Winter Fantasy from Huntington Beach?

About 24 miles. The Sawdust Art Festival is at 935 Laguna Canyon Road, Laguna Beach, roughly 35 minutes from north Huntington Beach with no traffic by OpenStreetMap routing. The fastest line uses a short stretch of the 73 toll road; staying on the 405 to Laguna Canyon Road (the 133) avoids tolls.
