Christmas Lights Near Huntington Beach 2026: Free Displays, Neighborhood Streets and Two Easy Evenings

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 20, 2026

Short answer: The free Christmas lights near Huntington Beach with confirmed 2026 dates are the Lighting of the Bay at Newport Dunes (nightly, November 27 to January 3), Fashion Island's tree snowfall show (November 20 to December 27), the Snoopy House in Costa Mesa (December 11 to 18) and the Pier and Main Street snowflakes (from December 6). Skip Naples Island this year: it's canceled.

The Snoopy House at Costa Mesa City Hall is open December 11 to 18, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. The Lighting of the Bay at Newport Dunes is free to view every night from November 27 to January 3, from dusk. Fashion Island's 90-foot tree has a snowfall show every half hour from 5:30 to 8 p.m., November 20 to December 27. On the Huntington Beach Pier and Main Street, the Light a Light of Love snowflakes go up on December 6 and stay lit through New Year's. Huntington Beach's best-known decorated streets are run by neighbors, not the city, and none has posted 2026 dates. Go after mid-December, on a weeknight, and treat them as a bonus. Skip Naples Island in Long Beach this year: its parade and decorating contest are canceled.

This guide is for families staying in Huntington Beach in December who want one or two evenings of lights that cost nothing and end in time for bed. Dates and times come from each organizer's own 2026 page, read on November 20, 2026. Where a 2026 date is not posted, we say so. Drive times are from our house in 92649 with light traffic, and they are rough.

Snoopy House, Costa Mesa (December 11 to 18)

This is the one to plan around if you have children under ten. The city's 2026 flyer marks 60 years of the display and lists December 11 to 18, 2026, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m., at Costa Mesa City Hall. Every night has the Snoopy lights, photos with Santa from 6 to 8 p.m., letters to Santa, stage performances and food vendors. Weekends add guest performers, train rides and play areas. The city's Snoopy House page says the light display stays open in bad weather, while the activities can be canceled, so check it on a wet day. It also points to sledding requirements and a stage schedule. Those were not readable on November 20, so look again before you go.

Seeing the display is free. Food, and anything a vendor sells, is not. Go on a weeknight (December 14 to 17) and arrive at 5:30 if you want short lines for Santa. It is the shortest run on this list: eight nights, ending the Friday before Christmas week. For a daytime Santa at the malls, see our Santa photos guide.

Newport: floating lights and a snowing tree

Lighting of the Bay at Newport Dunes. More than fifty lit decorations and trees float on the calm back bay. The Newport Dunes festival page lists the lights daily from November 27, 2026 to January 3, 2027, starting at dusk, rain or shine. The Fire & Lights Festival around it runs Thursday to Sunday evenings and is closed Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve. Fire dancers perform at 5:30 and 7:30 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays, and Santa visits on Saturday evenings from 5 to 8 through Christmas. Entry and the lights are free. Parking, food and the inflatables cost extra. Newport Dunes gives a flat $15 parking rate for opening night, Friday, November 27, when Santa switches the lights on at about 6 p.m. It does not post the parking price for other nights. Call 949-729-3863 if the price matters.

Fashion Island. In the Neiman Marcus to Bloomingdale's courtyard, the center's Nights of Coastal Lights page lists a 90-foot live tree that lights up with music and falling snow at 5:30, 6, 6:30, 7, 7:30 and 8 p.m. every night from November 20 to December 27, weather permitting. Time your arrival to the half hour.

Fashion Island and Newport Dunes are a short drive apart. One evening covers both: the 5:30 or 6 snowfall, then the bay by 7. Avoid December 16 to 20 for this route unless you also want the Newport Beach Christmas Boat Parade, which fills Newport's roads those nights. If you do want the parade, our Newport boat parade guide has the time the boats pass each free viewing spot.

Huntington Beach: the Pier snowflakes and the neighborhood streets

Light a Light of Love. Waymakers, an Orange County youth charity, lights sponsored snowflakes on the Huntington Beach Pier and Main Street. Its 2026 event page lists the lighting for Sunday, December 6, 2026 on Main Street and Pier Plaza, with each snowflake shining every evening through the New Year. No 2026 start time is posted yet. Any December evening after the 6th, walk Main Street and out along the Pier and back. Allow about an hour if you stop for food. Park in the Main Promenade structure. The first 90 minutes are free, and our downtown parking guide has the rest. On a Tuesday, the Surf City Nights street fair runs on Main Street from 4:30 to 8:30.

The neighborhood streets. These are private homes, and nobody publishes a schedule. The most complete local source is Enjoy OC, which keeps a best-neighborhoods page and a longer address list, both updated November 18, 2026 but built from past seasons. Near us, they name:

  • The Huntington Harbour islands: Davenport, Humboldt, Trinidad and Gilbert. They are a short drive from our house. Enjoy OC says you can see some of the lights from the street but the full effect is from a boat, because many homes decorate for the boat parades. That is what the Cruise of Lights (December 19 to 23) is for.
  • Meredith Gardens, around Brookhurst Street and Meredith Drive.
  • La Cresta Circle and Spinnaker Drive, off Brookhurst near Indianapolis Avenue.
  • Springdale Street and Brassie Lane.
  • Northwestern Way, just over the line in Westminster, where Enjoy OC says most of the houses decorate.

Be honest with the kids about what to expect. Enjoy OC found the Heil and Brookhurst neighborhood in Fountain Valley, historically a popular one, "fairly dark" on December 21, 2025. A street that was bright last year can be quiet this year. Treat these as a drive-by on the way home from the Pier, not the main event. We don't list single-house addresses, because the families living there did not ask for visitors.

Skip Naples Island in 2026

Naples in Long Beach is usually the walkable canal neighborhood people drive up for. This year the Naples Improvement Association has canceled the Naples Island Holiday Boat Parade and the neighborhood decorating contest. It cited tides of 7.5 to 8.5 feet expected in Christmas week and wanted to keep crowds off the island, according to the Long Beach Post report on LAist (November 7, 2026). Some residents will still decorate. With no contest and neighbors asking for fewer visitors, we would leave it alone this year. The same tides can flood parts of Pacific Coast Highway and Huntington Harbour on the mornings of December 23 to 25. Our king tides guide has the times.

How to look at lights without annoying the street

  • Go after December 10. Earlier, you will pass more houses that are not lit yet. Weeknights from Monday to Thursday are quieter than weekends, and the week before Christmas is the busiest.
  • Park and walk the residential streets. Park legally at the edge of the neighborhood, not across a driveway or in a red zone. Walk the block on the sidewalk and stay off lawns and away from cords.
  • If you drive through, keep moving. Use low beams, and pull over to stop rather than stopping in the lane. Don't idle with music playing in front of someone's house.
  • Keep the voices down, and be gone by about 9 p.m. People live there.
  • On a rainy night, displays may be off or unplugged. The Snoopy House and the Lighting of the Bay both say they stay lit in bad weather. Our rainy-day guide has indoor plans.

Two free evenings that work

Evening one, close to home (about 90 minutes): leave at 5:15, park downtown, and walk Main Street and the Pier under the snowflakes. Drive back through the Huntington Harbour islands on the way to the house. Any night from December 6.

Evening two, Costa Mesa and Newport (about 3 hours): Snoopy House at 5:30 for Santa photos, then Fashion Island for the 7 or 7:30 snowfall, then Newport Dunes for the floating lights. Monday, December 14 or Tuesday, December 15 is the best pair of nights. Newport's boat parade fills the roads from December 16 to 20. Before December 11, swap the Snoopy House for the Fashion Island and Dunes pair alone.

Want a ticketed night too? The Cruise of Lights in Huntington Harbour is the local one, and Knott's Merry Farm has a nightly tree lighting at 5:15. For a matinee before the Costa Mesa evening, see The Nutcracker and A Christmas Carol near Huntington Beach.

Christmas lights FAQ

What are the best free Christmas lights near Huntington Beach in 2026?

The ones with confirmed 2026 dates are the Snoopy House at Costa Mesa City Hall (December 11 to 18, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m.), the Lighting of the Bay at Newport Dunes (nightly from dusk, November 27 to January 3), the snowfall tree at Fashion Island (every half hour from 5:30 to 8 p.m., November 20 to December 27), and the Light a Light of Love snowflakes on the Huntington Beach Pier and Main Street from December 6 through New Year's.

When is the Costa Mesa Snoopy House in 2026?

The City of Costa Mesa's 2026 flyer lists December 11 to 18, 2026, from 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. at Costa Mesa City Hall, 77 Fair Drive. Photos with Santa run from 6 to 8 p.m. nightly. The light display stays open in bad weather, and the activities can be canceled.

Is the Newport Dunes Lighting of the Bay free?

Yes. Newport Dunes says the Lighting of the Bay is free to view all season, and entry to the Fire & Lights Festival is free. Parking, concessions and the inflatables cost extra. Opening-night parking on November 27, 2026 is a flat $15. Other nights' parking rates were not posted on November 20.

Which Huntington Beach neighborhoods have Christmas lights?

Enjoy OC's neighborhood lists, built from past seasons, name the Huntington Harbour islands (Davenport, Humboldt, Trinidad and Gilbert), Meredith Gardens near Brookhurst Street and Meredith Drive, La Cresta Circle and Spinnaker Drive near Brookhurst and Indianapolis, Springdale Street at Brassie Lane, and Northwestern Way just over the line in Westminster. These are private homes with no published schedule, so 2026 is not confirmed. Go after mid-December.

Is the Naples Island boat parade happening in 2026?

No. The Naples Improvement Association canceled the 2026 Naples Island Holiday Boat Parade and the decorating contest because of high tides expected in Christmas week, according to a November 7, 2026 Long Beach Post report on LAist.

When do the Huntington Beach Pier snowflake lights go up?

Waymakers lists its Light a Light of Love lighting for Sunday, December 6, 2026 on Main Street and Pier Plaza. The sponsored snowflakes stay lit each evening through the New Year. A 2026 start time for the lighting was not posted on November 20.
