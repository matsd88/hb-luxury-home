25 Best Things to Do in Huntington Beach (A Local Host’s Guide)

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated October 13, 2026

Huntington Beach isn't called “Surf City USA” for nothing, but there's much more here than waves. Here are our favorite ways to spend a few days in HB, from a host who lives here.

On the beach

  1. Walk the Huntington Beach Pier. At 1,850 feet, it's one of the longest on the West Coast. Go at sunset and watch the surfers below.
  2. Take a surf lesson. Beginner-friendly schools operate on both sides of the Pier year-round.
  3. Book a beach bonfire. Huntington State Beach and Bolsa Chica State Beach have first-come fire rings. Arrive early on weekends. See the Huntington Beach bonfire and fire pit guide for City Beach rings, reservations and the rules.
  4. Rent bikes on the beach path. The paved trail runs about 8.5 miles along the coast.
  5. Let the dog run at Dog Beach. North of Goldenwest Street.
  6. Watch a pro contest. The US Open of Surfing comes each summer.

Downtown & Main Street

  1. Surf City Nights. A Tuesday street fair with a farmers market, food and live music. Visiting for the holiday? It falls two days before Thanksgiving. See the Thanksgiving week guide.
  2. International Surfing Museum. Small but fun.
  3. Surfers' Hall of Fame. Handprints in cement at Main & PCH.
  4. Pacific City. An oceanfront shopping center with a food hall and rooftop views.

Nature & outdoors

  1. Bolsa Chica Ecological Reserve. A 1.5-mile flat loop with herons, egrets and pelicans.
  2. Huntington Central Park. Over 350 acres of trails, lakes, disc golf and an equestrian center.
  3. Shipley Nature Center. A quiet native-plant preserve inside Central Park.
  4. Paddle Huntington Harbour. Rent a SUP or kayak and cruise past waterfront homes.
  5. The Cruise of Lights. A December boat tour of the harbour's holiday displays. 2026 dates, shuttle, and the boat parade.

Eat & drink

  1. Breakfast burritos before a surf session.
  2. Fish tacos near the Pier.
  3. Sunset drinks at an oceanview rooftop.
  4. Local craft breweries around Gothard Street.
  5. Weekend brunch in Huntington Harbour.

Easy day trips

  1. Disneyland Resort, about 25 minutes. See our Disneyland day-trip guide.
  2. Knott's Berry Farm, about 20–25 minutes.
  3. Laguna Beach coves and galleries, about 35 minutes.
  4. Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach, about 25 minutes. In winter, pair it with a gray whale boat from the dock next door; see the whale-watching guide.
  5. Balboa Island & the Newport Fun Zone. Try a Balboa Bar.
