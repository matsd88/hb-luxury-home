What's Open on Christmas Day 2026 in Huntington Beach: Groceries, Dinner and Things to Do

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 16, 2026

Short answer: Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday. The beach, the Pier and the beach bike path keep their normal hours, and Disneyland was open on Christmas Day last year. Most places you would shop are closed: on December 25, 2025, Costco, Target, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, Walmart and Ralphs were all closed, while Vons and Albertsons stayed open (some with shorter hours), along with CVS and some 24-hour Walgreens. Do the big grocery run by Wednesday, December 23, and treat Christmas Eve as a short day: last year Costco closed at 5 p.m., most Trader Joe's at 5 and Target at 8. For Christmas dinner out, book an oceanfront hotel restaurant now. They are the most reliable Christmas Day tables in town.

This guide is for families and groups spending Christmas in a Huntington Beach vacation rental, especially if you fly in on the 23rd or 24th and arrive to an empty fridge. Chains post their 2026 holiday hours in December, so the store hours below are what each chain did on Christmas 2025, as reported by FOX 11 Los Angeles (Christmas Day) and Axios (Christmas Eve). Hours are set store by store, so call the one you plan to use. We checked everything on November 16, 2026.

The grocery plan: shop by the 23rd

  • Do the big shop on December 22 or 23. Christmas Eve is a Thursday this year and every store will be busy and closing early. Last year's Christmas Eve hours: Costco 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Target 7 a.m. to 8 p.m., Trader Joe's closing at about 5 p.m. (some lists said 6). Ralphs set hours by store (Axios).
  • Buy everything for the 25th and the 26th. Stores reopen on Saturday, December 26, but the morning after Christmas is busy too.
  • Your Christmas Day backup is a Vons or Albertsons, or CVS for basics like milk, coffee and batteries. Call first: some open late or close early.
  • Delivery apps can't help much. They deliver from stores, so on the 25th you get the same short list of open stores.
  • Buy firewood ahead if you want a beach bonfire on Christmas night. You have to bring your own, and the stores that sell it are closed. The bonfire guide has the fire ring rules and the 10 p.m. curfew.

Christmas dinner out: book a hotel restaurant

The four oceanfront resorts on Pacific Coast Highway are the safest bet for a Christmas Day meal, and they fill up. Here is what Visit Huntington Beach listed for Christmas 2025 (updated December 17, 2025):

  • Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach: a prix-fixe Christmas menu at Watertable, 3 to 8 p.m.
  • The Waterfront Beach Resort: a Christmas brunch at Cabo Wabo Beach Club, seatings hourly from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., $155 for adults, $65 for kids 4 to 12 and free for 3 and under, service charge included, with photos with Mr. and Mrs. Claus. A three-course Christmas dinner ran 5 to 9 p.m. there and at Henry's Coastal Cuisine.
  • Paséa Hotel & Spa: Christmas dining at Lōrea on December 24 and 25.
  • Kimpton Shorebreak: a Christmas menu at Pacific Hideaway on December 24 and 25.

None of them has posted 2026 menus or prices yet, so treat these as a guide to what to expect and book directly with the hotel. Independent restaurants on Main Street decide one by one whether to open. Many close, so call before you walk down.

Things to do on Christmas Day that are actually open

  • Skip the beach in the morning. Christmas morning brings a king tide: NOAA predicts a 7.0-foot high tide at 9:15 a.m. at the Newport Bay entrance, and the sand nearly disappears. In October, an extra-high tide flooded Pacific Coast Highway through Sunset Beach. The king tides guide has the times and the roads to avoid.
  • Go in the late afternoon instead. The tide drops to a low at about 4:37 p.m., just before the sun sets at about 4:50. That is the best hour for a walk or a family photo; see the family photos guide.
  • Walk Bolsa Chica. The Ecological Reserve is a free, flat wetlands walk with free parking; the Conservancy says the trails run sunrise to sunset. See the Bolsa Chica guide.
  • Ride or walk the beach path. It is paved and follows the beach, so it keeps beach hours.
  • Disneyland, about 25 minutes from the house in light traffic, if you can take the crowds. The kids and Disneyland guide has the plan.
  • A movie. Many theaters run showtimes on Christmas Day; check the listing for the theater you want.

The week around Christmas has more going on: the Cruise of Lights runs December 19 to 23, the Newport Beach Christmas Boat Parade sails December 16 to 20 (free from shore), and the New Year's Eve guide covers the end of the week.

Small things that shift that week

  • Shipping gifts: for delivery before Christmas, USPS recommends sending by December 17 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, December 18 for Priority Mail and December 19 for Priority Mail Express (USPS 2026 holiday shipping dates). These are recommendations, not guarantees. Before you ship anything to a vacation rental, ask the host if packages can be delivered there and when.
  • Trash day: Republic Services, the city's hauler, lists Christmas Day as an observed holiday, and collection is delayed one day (Huntington Beach service guide).
  • Getting around: OC Bus runs a Sunday schedule, so buses come less often. If you drive to the beach, the meters and pay stations still charge; the parking guide has the lots.
  • If someone gets sick: call 911 for emergencies. Hospital emergency rooms are open; urgent care clinics often keep holiday hours, so call before you drive over.

What we can't tell you yet

  • 2026 store hours. As of November 16, the chains above have not posted Christmas 2026 hours. Everything in the table is from 2025.
  • 2026 hotel menus and prices. Visit Huntington Beach usually updates its Christmas dining list in December.
  • Whether Surf City Nights runs on December 22. The Tuesday street fair on Main Street has no Christmas-week cancellation posted, but no confirmation either.
  • Individual restaurants. We have not called every restaurant in town. If one matters to you, call it.

Christmas Day FAQ

Which grocery stores are open on Christmas Day in Huntington Beach?

On Christmas Day 2025, Vons and Albertsons were open (some with reduced hours), along with CVS and select 24-hour Walgreens. Ralphs, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, Costco, Target and Walmart were closed. Chains post 2026 hours in December, so call your store.

Is Trader Joe's open on Christmas Day?

No. Trader Joe's closed on December 25, 2025, and most stores closed at about 5 p.m. on Christmas Eve. Shop by December 23 if you can.

Is Disneyland open on Christmas Day?

It was in 2025: Disneyland Park ran 8 a.m. to midnight and Disney California Adventure 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Christmas week is one of the busiest of the year. Check the resort's calendar for 2026 hours.

Is the beach open on Christmas Day in Huntington Beach?

Yes. Huntington City Beach is open 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. and Huntington State Beach 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., with no Christmas closure listed. Parking meters still charge. A 7.0-foot king tide is predicted at 9:15 a.m., so go in the late afternoon.

Do OC buses run on Christmas Day?

Yes. OCTA runs OC Bus on its Sunday/holiday schedule on Christmas Day.

Where can we eat Christmas dinner in Huntington Beach?

The oceanfront hotels are the most reliable. For Christmas 2025, Visit Huntington Beach listed Watertable at the Hyatt Regency (3 to 8 p.m.), a brunch and dinner at The Waterfront Beach Resort, Lōrea at Paséa and Pacific Hideaway at Kimpton Shorebreak. Book directly; 2026 menus are not posted yet.
