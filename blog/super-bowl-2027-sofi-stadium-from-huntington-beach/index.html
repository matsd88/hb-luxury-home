Super Bowl LXI 2027 from Huntington Beach: Where to Stay and How to Get to SoFi

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated October 28, 2026

Short answer: Super Bowl LXI is on Sunday, February 14, 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. That is about 31 miles from our house in Huntington Beach, roughly 45 minutes up the I-405 with no traffic. On game day, plan on 90 minutes or more. Huntington Beach works as a base if the game is one day of a longer trip, or if half your group is coming for the beach rather than the football. If you only want to sleep, eat and walk to the stadium, stay closer to Inglewood or LAX. The kickoff time and general ticket sale had not been announced as of October 28, 2026.

This guide is for a fan, or a family with one fan in it, planning a long weekend in Southern California around the game. Facts come from SoFi Stadium's Super Bowl LXI page, its parking and transportation page, the Los Angeles Super Bowl Host Committee and Metro's SoFi Stadium page, all checked October 28, 2026. Drive distances are routed from our house in 92649.

Is Huntington Beach a good base for the Super Bowl?

It depends on what the rest of the weekend looks like. Hotels around Inglewood, LAX and the stadium district are the obvious choice, and they are also the ones that fill and reprice first for a Super Bowl. From Huntington Beach you trade a longer ride on game day for a quieter, beach-town weekend the other two days.

  • Good fit: a group where not everyone has a ticket. The fans go to Inglewood, everyone else spends Sunday at the beach, and you watch the second half together. Or a family that turns the game into a Presidents' Day weekend, with whale watching, a surf lesson or a Disneyland day around it.
  • Also a good fit: fans without tickets. If you are coming for the atmosphere and will watch on a screen anyway, a beach town with a big TV is a better Sunday than a hotel room near a stadium you cannot get into.
  • Poor fit: anyone going to several Super Bowl Week events in Los Angeles. NFL Honors, the Super Bowl Experience and other events will be spread around the region, and their venues and dates had not been posted as of October 28, 2026. If you plan to be in LA every night, stay in LA.

The weekend before is busy here too. The Surf City Marathon runs along PCH in Huntington Beach on Sunday, February 7, 2027, so a two-week stay can cover both.

Getting from Huntington Beach to SoFi on game day

Option 1: Drive, with a parking pass

This is the most direct option if your ticket package includes parking or you can buy a pass through the official channels. SoFi's own rules, which apply to every event:

  • Every car needs a pass. SoFi says all guests must have a parking pass, one pass covers one space, and guests without one are sent offsite. For NFL games it says onsite parking is "extremely limited," and event-day passes "may be available but limited" and cost more.
  • Lots open early. For NFL games, lots open 4 to 5 hours before kickoff and entry gates 2 to 3 hours before. The Super Bowl will have its own schedule and security, so check the instructions that come with your tickets.
  • Card only at the gates, and no refunds for lost or unused passes.
  • Timing from the beach: the drive is about 31 miles, mostly the I-405 north, then local streets into Inglewood. It takes roughly 45 minutes with no traffic. On Super Bowl Sunday, allow at least 90 minutes, and more if you want to be inside when the gates open.

Option 2: Rideshare

SoFi's rideshare drop-off is on the northbound curb lane of Kareem Court, and pickup is at Kareem Court and Manchester Boulevard. A ride from Huntington Beach is a long fare, and after the game everyone in the stadium is trying to get a car from the same corner. Walking a few blocks away before you request, or waiting out the first rush, usually helps. Bird and Lime scooters are not allowed in Inglewood, so there is no scooter shortcut.

Option 3: Park at a Metro station and ride the C Line

This is the car-light plan from our side of the county. Drive about 20 miles (around 30 minutes) to Lakewood Bl station in Downey. Metro says parking there is free on weekends and holidays. Ride the C Line west to its last stop, LAX/Metro Transit Center, without changing trains. The fare is $1.75.

For Rams and Chargers games, Metro runs the SoFi Stadium Express from Bus Bay 8 at LAX/Metro Transit Center, starting 3 hours before kickoff and running until 90 minutes after the game. Metro's page says the shuttle is "available for Rams and Chargers Games" and does not mention the Super Bowl. Check metro.net/sofi in early February 2027 before you rely on it.

Tickets: what is official

  • On Location: SoFi Stadium's event page calls On Location, the NFL's official hospitality company, "the only official source for Super Bowl LXI ticket and hospitality packages." Packages are expensive, and they come with guaranteed seats.
  • NFL Ticket Exchange on Ticketmaster: the NFL calls it the league's only authorized resale marketplace, and it says tickets sold there are validated by Ticketmaster and the NFL.
  • Everything else: the NFL warns that you cannot confirm a ticket from anyone else is real until you try to get in. Do not buy from people on the street or outside the stadium.

Buy the ticket before you book anything you cannot cancel. Airbnb stays have a cancellation policy you see before you book; read it with the game in mind.

If you are watching, not going

Most people who come to Southern California for Super Bowl weekend will not be inside the stadium. A Sunday in Huntington Beach is still a good way to spend it: the beach in the morning, an early lunch, then the game. February is a cool-season month here, so bring a layer for the evening; the winter weather guide shows what that season is like at the coast. February is also gray whale season (NOAA says the northbound trip with calves starts in mid-February), which the whale watching guide covers. Main Street and Pacific City restaurants and bars will be busy around kickoff, so if you want a table for the game, book or arrive early.

Super Bowl from Huntington Beach FAQ

When is Super Bowl LXI and where is it played?

Sunday, February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium, 1001 S. Stadium Drive in Inglewood. Kickoff time had not been announced as of October 28, 2026; recent Super Bowls have kicked off at about 3:30 p.m. Pacific. It is the second Super Bowl at SoFi, after Super Bowl LVI in February 2022.

How far is SoFi Stadium from Huntington Beach?

About 31 miles from our house in Huntington Beach 92649, mostly on the I-405 north. That is roughly 45 minutes with no traffic. On a Super Bowl Sunday, with road closures and security around the stadium, plan on at least 90 minutes.

Can I park at SoFi Stadium on Super Bowl Sunday?

Only with a pass, and they are limited. SoFi says all guests must have a parking pass, that onsite parking for NFL events is extremely limited, and that event-day parking may not be available. Buy a pass through the official ticket channels when you buy your seats, and never from a stranger.

Can I take Metro to SoFi Stadium from Huntington Beach?

Partly. Drive about 20 miles to Lakewood Bl station in Downey, where Metro says weekend and holiday parking is free, then ride the C Line to LAX/Metro Transit Center. For Rams and Chargers games Metro runs the SoFi Stadium Express shuttle from Bus Bay 8 there. Metro had not said whether it runs for the Super Bowl as of October 28, 2026.

Where do you buy Super Bowl LXI tickets?

SoFi Stadium lists On Location, the NFL's official hospitality company, as the only official source for Super Bowl LXI ticket and hospitality packages so far. The NFL Ticket Exchange on Ticketmaster is the league's authorized resale marketplace. General sale dates had not been announced as of October 28, 2026.
