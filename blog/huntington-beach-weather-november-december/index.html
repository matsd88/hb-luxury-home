Huntington Beach in November and December: Weather, Water Temperature and Sunset Times

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated October 24, 2026

Short answer: November in Huntington Beach is mild and mostly dry. At the beach, the average high is about 68°F and the average low about 55°F, with only about 1.6 days of meaningful rain in the month. The ocean is usually somewhere around 60–66°F: fine for a quick swim if you run warm, but most surfers wear a full wetsuit. The big change is the light. Clocks go back on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and sunset jumps from 6:02 p.m. to 5:01 p.m. By Thanksgiving it is 4:45 p.m. Plan beach time for midday, pack a warm layer for after dark, and treat sunset as the evening's main event.

November and December at a glance

Air temperatures and rain are NOAA's official 1991–2020 climate normals for the Newport Beach Harbor station, the closest long-running station on the coast. Ocean temperatures are November and December averages from NOAA buoy 46253 (San Pedro South), about 12 miles off the coast, for 2023 to 2025. Sunset times are for the Huntington Beach Pier, worked out with NOAA's solar equations, and may differ by a minute from your weather app. All checked October 24, 2026.

"Rainy day" here means a day with at least a tenth of an inch. Ten minutes inland it is warmer by day and cooler at night: NOAA's normals for Long Beach Airport are 73°F / 52°F for November.

Can you swim in November?

You can, and on a sunny afternoon some people do. Whether you will enjoy it depends on the year. The buoy's November average was 64.9°F in 2023, 60.3°F in 2024 and 65.8°F in 2025, and single readings ranged from 57.6°F to 68.7°F. So "around 60–66°F" is honest, and a cold year really does feel colder. This week runs warm: the buoy read 73°F on the afternoon of October 24, 2026. Water in the surf zone can be cooler than the offshore buoy, so wade in before you commit.

  • Kids splashing at the edge: fine on a warm afternoon, with a towel and a dry layer waiting.
  • Swimming laps or bodysurfing: most people want at least a spring suit, and many a full 3/2mm wetsuit.
  • Surf lessons: schools include a wetsuit. The learn-to-surf guide has prices and wetsuit thickness by season.

Lifeguards: California State Parks says Huntington State Beach has lifeguard services throughout the year, and warns of strong rip currents there. Swim near a staffed tower or the Pier, and ask a lifeguard about conditions before you go in.

The November 1 time change

Daylight saving time ends at 2 a.m. on the first Sunday of November (NIST), which is November 1, 2026. Your phone changes by itself. What changes for a beach trip:

  • Sunset is around 4:45 to 5:00 p.m. all month. If you want sunset at the Pier, leave the house by about 4 p.m. and use the parking guide to pick a lot.
  • Mornings get lighter. Sunrise moves to about 6:10 a.m., which helps early surf sessions and dog walks inland.
  • Bonfires start earlier. Huntington State Beach's fire rings are open year-round, 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., first come, first served, and the entry gates close at 9 p.m. In November it is dark before 5:30 p.m., so you get a long fire night. The bonfire guide covers which beach to pick.

Rain and Santa Ana days

Most November days are dry. When it does rain, Orange County's health agency posts rain advisories at ocbeachinfo.com, and lifeguards such as Seal Beach Marine Safety recommend staying out of the ocean for 72 hours after significant rain, because runoff raises bacteria levels. The rainy-day guide has indoor plans for that window.

The other fall weather is the opposite: Santa Ana winds, the hot, dry offshore winds that Cal OES says typically blow from October to December. Those days are warm, the air is very dry, and fire danger goes up. Check the National Weather Service forecast for Huntington Beach before a bonfire night.

What to pack for Huntington Beach in November

  • Day: shorts or light pants, T-shirts, sunglasses and sunscreen. Afternoons near 68°F in the sun feel warm.
  • Evening: a fleece or light jacket and long pants. Lows near 55°F at the coast feel colder on the sand with a breeze.
  • Beach: swimsuit, sandals and a beach towel. Bring or rent a wetsuit if you plan to be in the water for long.
  • Skip: a heavy winter coat. An umbrella is optional in November and more useful in December.

Is November a good time to visit?

For most groups, yes. Summer crowds are gone, beach parking is on off-season rates, and dry, mild weather is the norm. It suits people who want to walk the Pier, bike the beach trail, watch the surf and eat on Main Street more than people who want long swims in warm water. That is late summer and early fall. On the calendar: Surf City Nights on Main Street every Tuesday from 4:30 to 8:30 p.m., Darker Waves at Huntington State Beach on November 14 (see the Darker Waves stay guide), and Thanksgiving on November 26 (see the Thanksgiving week guide). December brings the Cruise of Lights and the start of gray whale season.

What we cannot tell you

Climate normals are 30-year averages, not a forecast. Any given week can be 10 degrees warmer or colder. The buoy sits offshore, and the water at the shore can differ by a few degrees, especially after a windy spell. Check the National Weather Service forecast the week you travel.

November weather FAQ

How warm is Huntington Beach in November?

At the coast, the average high is about 68°F and the average low about 55°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals, Newport Beach Harbor). A few miles inland, highs are a little warmer and nights a little cooler.

What is the water temperature in Huntington Beach in November?

Usually around 60–66°F. NOAA buoy 46253 offshore averaged 64.9°F in November 2023, 60.3°F in 2024 and 65.8°F in 2025. Most surfers wear a full wetsuit.

What time is sunset in Huntington Beach in November?

After clocks go back on November 1, 2026, sunset is 5:01 p.m. It drops to about 4:50 p.m. by mid-month and 4:45 p.m. on Thanksgiving, November 26.

Does it rain in Huntington Beach in November?

Not much. NOAA's normal is 0.64 inches for the month, with about 1.6 days of at least a tenth of an inch. December is wetter, at about 1.6 inches. Stay out of the ocean for 72 hours after significant rain.

Are there lifeguards in Huntington Beach in November?

Yes. California State Parks lists lifeguard services at Huntington State Beach year-round. Swim near a staffed tower or the Pier.
