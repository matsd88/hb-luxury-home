Laguna Beach Day Trip from Huntington Beach (2026 Guide)

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated October 26, 2026

Short answer: Take Pacific Coast Highway south and park once. From north Huntington Beach (92649) to Main Beach, OpenStreetMap with no traffic is about 24 miles and 40 minutes on PCH, signed West Coast Highway through Newport Beach. The freeway into Laguna Canyon Road is about 24 miles and 35 minutes. Through June 25, 2027, the coastal trolley is free, the Glenneyre Structure is $3 an hour with a 3-hour limit, and Lot 16 is $5 on weekends. Take nothing from the tide pools.

The drive on PCH

Those times are with no traffic, from the 92649 neighborhood to Main Beach at Broadway and Coast Highway. The road passes Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, Corona del Mar, and Crystal Cove. For a day in town, see things to do in Huntington Beach. For a boat day, see the Catalina Island day trip.

Parking and the trolley

The city posts this off-season service from September 8, 2026 through June 25, 2027. Summer Breeze, the Irvine park-and-ride, ended September 6, 2026. Use the Laguna Beach Trolley app.

On a weekday, park downtown and ride the coastal trolley. The Glenneyre Structure, 501 Glenneyre Street, lists 208 spaces at $3 an hour with a 3-hour limit. The Broadway lot, 234 Broadway, is the same rate. Pay by card, cash, or the Laguna Beach Parking App. Lot pages say enforcement is 8 a.m.–7 p.m. The parking page says 8 a.m.–9 p.m. Read the sign. A disabled placard parks free at meters and in city lots for up to 72 hours.

Coves and beaches

Main Beach Park at Broadway and Coast Highway has a playground modeled on the lifeguard tower, basketball and volleyball, restrooms, and an accessible stair. Leashed dogs are allowed before 9 a.m. and after 6 p.m. from June 15 through September 10, and any time the rest of the year.

Heisler Park, 375 Cliff Drive, is the bluff at the north end of the trolley, with barbecues, picnic tables, restrooms, and a gazebo. Rockpile, about 700 feet, has tide pools on a lower tide. Bird Rock is off limits. High tide leaves almost no sand. Swimming and bodyboarding are not permitted, urchins cover the reef, and fishing is not allowed.

Crescent Bay has restrooms, showers, and skimboarding. Crescent Bay Point on Crescent Bay Drive is the panoramic sunset bluff. Victoria Beach is for body surfing, tide pooling, and skimboarding south at Lagunita. There is no public restroom, fishing is not allowed, the city says children should avoid the rips, and the north rocks are unsafe in surf or at high tide. Thousand Steps has restrooms at the bottom of the stairs. Rips are always present and worse in large surf. The trolley runs on toward Mission Hospital.

Tide-pool rules

Fish and Wildlife lists two no-take areas from 2012. The reserve from Abalone Point to Goff Island is 6.72 square miles and 4.4 miles of shore. The no-take conservation area from Goff Island to Table Rock is 3.09 square miles. Taking any living, geological, or cultural marine resource is unlawful, including fishing. Swimming and surfing are allowed. Marine Safety says fishing is open north of Abalone Point and south of Seacliff Drive. The city's rules for Main Beach, Shaw's Cove, and Treasure Island: do not collect animals, shells, or rocks; do not touch animals; do not step on them; do not turn rocks over. Go on a low tide; our winter low-tide calendar for tide pools near Huntington Beach lists the best afternoons and covers Little Corona and Crystal Cove on the way down.

Galleries, First Thursdays, and food

Laguna Art Museum, 307 Cliff Drive, is open Tuesday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Monday except some holidays, plus New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. Admission is $15, $10 for students 20 and older, seniors, and military, and free for 19 and under with an adult. Use meters on Cliff Drive or Coast Highway.

First Thursdays Art Walk is free the first Thursday, 6 to 9 p.m., on Coast Highway from Diamond Street to Myrtle Street. The museum is usually free 6 to 9 p.m. that night. On November 5, 2026, free entry is only 5 to 6 p.m. for one exhibition, then a registered talk. The Thursday city trolley ends at 6 p.m. Visit Laguna Beach lists December 3, 2026 and January 7, 2027. The Art Walk says it adds free trolley trips and does not list the last one.

The Saturday farmers market is 8 a.m. to noon at 521 Forest Avenue. Las Brisas, 361 Cliff Drive, lists Sunday–Thursday 8 a.m.–9 p.m. and Friday–Saturday 8 a.m.–10 p.m.

A sample day

Check-in is 4:00 p.m. and checkout is 11:00 a.m., so use a middle day. Leave at 9 a.m. Weekend parking is Lot 16. Weekday parking is Glenneyre. Then Main Beach, Heisler, and either Crescent Bay or a south trolley to Victoria Beach and Thousand Steps. Eat at the Saturday market before noon or at Las Brisas. Sunset is at Crescent Bay Point or Heisler, then PCH north in daylight. On a first Thursday, save the galleries for 6 p.m.

Tips

  • Park once. The downtown limit is 3 hours.
  • Lot 16 is closed to the public Monday through Friday.
  • Do not swim at Rockpile, and stay off Bird Rock.
  • Checked October 26, 2026. After June 25, 2027, use the summer timetable.

Laguna Beach day-trip FAQ

How long is the drive from Huntington Beach to Laguna Beach?

About 24 miles and 40 minutes to Main Beach on PCH with no traffic, from 92649. The freeway into Laguna Canyon Road is about 35 minutes.

Is the Laguna Beach trolley free?

Yes, from September 8, 2026 through June 25, 2027. Weekend Lot 16 parking is $5 and the canyon ride is free.

Where should I park for a Laguna Beach day?

Weekends: Lot 16, 1900 Laguna Canyon Road, $5, then the trolley. Weekdays: Glenneyre Structure, 501 Glenneyre Street, $3 an hour, 3-hour limit.

Can I take shells or rocks from Laguna Beach tide pools?

No. Both areas are no-take. Leave animals, shells, and rocks, and do not touch them or turn rocks.

When is First Thursdays Art Walk?

The first Thursday, 6 to 9 p.m., free. Confirm the extra trolley. The regular Thursday trolley ends at 6 p.m. November 5, 2026 has shorter museum hours.
