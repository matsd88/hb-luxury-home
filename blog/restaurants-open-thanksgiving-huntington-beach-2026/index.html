Restaurants Open on Thanksgiving 2026 in Huntington Beach: Prices, Hours and Where to Book

By Mats, SuperhostUpdated November 23, 2026

Short answer: Yes, you can still eat out on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, 2026, in Huntington Beach. The cheapest posted dinner is Duke's by the Pier: $37 for adults, $17 for kids 10 and under, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. King's Fish House at Bella Terra serves a $55 three-course menu ($19 for kids) all day. The beach resorts run buffets from $145 (Hyatt) to $172 (Waterfront). Book today.

This guide is for a family or group staying in Huntington Beach for Thanksgiving who decided, a few days out, that nobody wants to cook in a rental kitchen. Every hour and price below comes from the restaurant's own page or menu, or from Visit Huntington Beach's official Thanksgiving list, read on November 23, 2026. Where something is not posted, we say so. Prices are before tax and tip unless noted.

What does Thanksgiving dinner at Duke's Huntington Beach cost?

Duke's posts a plated Thanksgiving dinner for $37 per adult and $17 per child 10 and under, served 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Thursday, November 26. It is the lowest posted price on the oceanfront.

The Duke's menus page links a Thanksgiving menu PDF. The plate is free-range Diestel turkey with Portuguese sausage stuffing, cranberry sauce, seasonal vegetables, Yukon Gold mashed potatoes, sweet potatoes and pan gravy. The PDF does not say whether the regular menu is also served that day, and it does not mention tax or gratuity.

  • Where: 317 Pacific Coast Highway, on the sand next to the Huntington Beach Pier.
  • Book: the site says reservations are recommended for the dining room. Use the OpenTable link on dukeshuntington.com or call 714-374-6446.
  • Parking: Duke's says there is valet and metered self-parking in the public lot on the west side of PCH, just south of the Pier. Our Pier and Main Street parking guide has the rates and the other lots.

Is King's Fish House at Bella Terra open on Thanksgiving?

Yes. The King's Fish House Huntington Beach page says it is hosting Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26, with a special three-course menu all day alongside the full menu. That makes it the easy pick for a group where not everyone wants turkey.

The Thanksgiving menu is $55 per person, or $19 for the children's menu (12 and under), with tax and gratuity not included:

  • Starter: New England clam chowder or a mixed green salad.
  • Dinner: roasted Diestel Family Ranch turkey with garlic mashed potatoes, sourdough apple-celery stuffing, turkey gravy and cranberry sauce.
  • Dessert: pumpkin cheesecake or chocolate cake.

It is at 7691 Edinger Avenue in the Bella Terra center, phone 714-793-1177, with reservations through the link on its page. It is inland, so there is no ocean view.

Which Huntington Beach hotels have a Thanksgiving buffet in 2026?

All four oceanfront resorts are serving on November 26, 2026, according to Visit Huntington Beach. Two are buffets in a ballroom with fixed seatings. The other two are restaurant dinners served most of the day.

  • Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach, Thanksgiving Feast: noon to 4 p.m. in the Huntington Ballroom. $145 for adults, $75 for ages 4 to 12, free for 3 and under. Sales tax and a 20% gratuity are not included, and a $50 deposit is required. Book on Tock or at 714-845-4659.
  • The Waterfront Beach Resort, Thanksgiving by the Sea: a holiday brunch from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. in the Breakwater Ballroom and on the Vista Lawns. $172 for adults and $70 for ages 4 to 12. Tickets are sold through the resort's website.
  • Kimpton Shorebreak, Pacific Hideaway: Thanksgiving dinner from 11 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. with herb-roasted turkey, sides, house-made cornbread and pumpkin pie. $85 to $130 per person, with no separate child price listed. Reservations are required on OpenTable or at 714-965-4448.
  • Paséa Hotel, Lōrea: Thanksgiving dining from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. No menu or price is listed. Book on OpenTable or at 714-698-6130.

When we checked on November 23, the hotels' own websites did not show these Thanksgiving pages, so confirm the price on the booking screen before you pay a deposit.

What about Thanksgiving dinner in Newport Beach?

Two long-running Newport Beach restaurants have posted Thanksgiving menus. Both are about 20 to 25 minutes down PCH from Huntington Beach without traffic.

  • Five Crowns, Corona del Mar: the Five Crowns Thanksgiving page lists dine-in from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. on November 26, 2026, with roast turkey and prime rib. The page does not list a price. 3801 E. Coast Highway, (949) 760-0331. Reservations are recommended.
  • Rusty Pelican, Newport Beach: the Rusty Pelican Thanksgiving menu lists a turkey dinner at $39 ($14 for children) and a 12-ounce prime rib dinner at $65 ($23 for children) on Thursday, November 26. Hours are not on that page. 2735 W. Coast Highway, (949) 642-3431.

Is there a casual or later option on Thanksgiving?

Buca di Beppo Huntington Beach is open for dine-in from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day, according to its Thanksgiving page. It is family-style Italian, so it suits a big group that wants to share plates. The page does not list a dine-in Thanksgiving menu or prices. It is at 7979 Center Avenue, (714) 891-4666.

The same page sells turkey feasts to go for 2 to 3 or 4 to 6 people, with pickup from November 23 to 26 and Thanksgiving Day pickup from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. It says availability is limited and does not list prices or an order deadline. For grocery-store turkey dinners, see our Thanksgiving dinner to-go guide.

How do we plan Thanksgiving Day around a dinner reservation?

Book an early-afternoon table and keep the morning for the beach. Sunset is around 4:45 p.m. that week, so a 1 or 2 p.m. meal leaves time for a walk on the sand before dark.

  1. Morning: the Turkey Wobble 5K and 10K start in waves from 7 to 9 a.m. at PCH and Beach Boulevard. Our Thanksgiving week guide has the details.
  2. Book now: use the restaurant's own link or phone number. If online booking shows nothing for your party size, call the number listed.
  3. Meal: a ballroom buffet runs on fixed hours (the Hyatt's ends at 4 p.m. and the Waterfront's at 2 p.m.), so do not book the last slot.
  4. Sunset: the Pier is a short walk from Duke's, Pacific Hideaway and Lōrea. The November weather guide has sunset times and water temperature.

What we could not confirm

  • Availability. None of these pages says whether it is sold out. Three days out, expect limited times, especially for groups of 6 or more.
  • Hotel pages. The four resort events come from Visit Huntington Beach's list. We could not find them on the hotels' own websites on November 23.
  • Lōrea's and Buca's prices, and Rusty Pelican's Thanksgiving hours. None was posted.
  • Henry's Coastal Cuisine at the Waterfront had no 2026 Thanksgiving menu posted.

Thanksgiving restaurants in Huntington Beach FAQ

Which restaurants in Huntington Beach are open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Duke's at the Pier (11 a.m. to 7 p.m.), King's Fish House at Bella Terra (all day) and Buca di Beppo (11 a.m. to 8 p.m.) have posted Thanksgiving Day hours for Thursday, November 26, 2026. All four oceanfront resorts also serve: the Hyatt Regency, the Waterfront Beach Resort, Pacific Hideaway at the Kimpton Shorebreak and Lōrea at the Paséa.

How much is Thanksgiving dinner at Duke's Huntington Beach in 2026?

Duke's Thanksgiving dinner is $37 for adults and $17 for kids 10 and under, served 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. on November 26, 2026. It is a plated turkey dinner with Portuguese sausage stuffing, mashed potatoes, sweet potatoes, vegetables, cranberry sauce and gravy. Reservations are recommended at 714-374-6446.

Which Huntington Beach hotels have a Thanksgiving buffet?

The Hyatt Regency has a Thanksgiving Feast from noon to 4 p.m. ($145 adults, $75 ages 4 to 12, plus tax and a 20% gratuity, with a $50 deposit). The Waterfront Beach Resort has a Thanksgiving by the Sea brunch from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. ($172 adults, $70 ages 4 to 12), per Visit Huntington Beach.

Is King's Fish House open on Thanksgiving?

Yes. King's Fish House at Bella Terra, 7691 Edinger Avenue, serves a three-course Thanksgiving menu all day on November 26, 2026, alongside its full menu. It is $55 per person and $19 for kids 12 and under, with tax and gratuity not included.

Can I get Thanksgiving dinner to go on Thanksgiving Day in Huntington Beach?

Buca di Beppo Huntington Beach lists Thanksgiving Day pickup from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. for its turkey feasts for 2 to 3 or 4 to 6 people, with limited availability and no posted prices. Grocery-store turkey dinners mostly need to be ordered days ahead.
