Local favorites

  • Bolsa Chica Ecological Reserve. An easy, flat loop trail with great birding at sunrise.
  • Surfing lessons at the Pier. Several local schools offer beginner lessons year-round.
  • Surf City Nights. A Tuesday-evening street fair and farmers market on Main Street.
  • Beach bonfires. Fire rings line Huntington State Beach and Bolsa Chica State Beach.
  • Huntington Central Park. Trails, a sports complex, a library and a dog park.
  • Huntington Harbour. Paddleboarding, kayaking and the holiday Cruise of Lights.
